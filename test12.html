文法重點
重點一：hard / hardly 和 late / lately 的易混淆用法

hard 作副詞時表示「努力地；用力地」，hardly 則表示「幾乎不」，兩者意義截然不同。同樣，late 當副詞表示「晚；遲」，而 lately 表示「最近；近來」。

She worked hard to pass the exam. 她努力用功以通過考試。
He hardly studied, so he failed. 他幾乎沒念書，所以不及格。

The train arrived late again today. 火車今天又晚到了。
Lately, the weather has been unusually cold. 最近天氣異常寒冷。

重點二：No sooner...than 的倒裝句型

No sooner + had + S + p.p. + than + S + V(過去式)，表示「一…就…」。No sooner 置於句首時，主詞與助動詞需倒裝。此句型強調兩件事發生的時間極為接近。

No sooner had she sat down than the phone rang. 她一坐下，電話就響了。
（正常語序：She had no sooner sat down than the phone rang.）

類似句型：Hardly/Scarcely had S + p.p. when/before S + V（過去式）
Hardly had he left the house when it started to rain. 他一離開家就開始下雨了。

重點三：非限定關係子句 (Non-restrictive Relative Clause) 的使用

非限定關係子句以逗點 + which/who/whom 引導，補充說明先行詞，去掉此子句不影響主句意思。注意：非限定子句不能用 that 替代 which。

Mount Jade is the highest peak in Taiwan, which attracts many hikers each year.
玉山是台灣最高的山峰，每年吸引許多登山者。（補充說明玉山）

He donated all his savings to charity, which surprised everyone around him.
他把所有積蓄捐給慈善機構，這讓周圍的人都感到驚訝。（which 代指前整件事）

閱讀測驗

In today's fast-changing job market, the ability to adapt and grow has become just as important as technical skills. Many employers now look beyond academic merit when making hiring decisions. They seek candidates who show dedication to continuous learning and who can proceed through challenges without giving up.

When companies recruit new staff, they often value qualities such as creativity, communication, and a positive attitude. A flawless academic record may catch attention, but it is rarely enough on its own. An appealing candidate is one who has real-world experience and can demonstrate an initial understanding of the company's goals and culture.

Research shows that workers who commute long distances often report lower job satisfaction. As a result, more organizations are allowing employees to work remotely. This shift has had a significant impact on productivity and employee well-being. Companies that periodically review their work policies and adapt to employee needs tend to prosper in the long run.

There is also a growing tendency among professionals to switch careers in pursuit of greater fulfillment. When a person's skills are no longer valued, they may be replaced or asked to take on new roles. The key to lasting success is the willingness to learn, to illustrate one's value through action, and to earn recognition through consistent hard work. Those who triumph in their careers are often those who combine talent with a strong work ethic and an eye for accuracy in everything they do.

單字選擇題複習（5 題）
1. After years of working hard, the young entrepreneur finally saw her business begin to _____ in the competitive market.
(A) recruit
(B) prosper
(C) commute
(D) replace
答案▼
(B) prosper — 在競爭市場中「繁榮、興旺」，符合語境。
2. The company decided to _____ its old software system with a more efficient, cloud-based solution.
(A) illustrate
(B) proceed
(C) replace
(D) elaborate
答案▼
(C) replace — replace B with A，用新系統取代舊系統。
3. The professor asked the student to _____ on her research findings in more detail during the presentation.
(A) triumph
(B) elaborate
(C) appeal
(D) dedicate
答案▼
(B) elaborate — elaborate on sth. 表示「詳細說明某事」。
4. The scientist's detailed _____ of animal behavior in the wild led to a groundbreaking new theory.
(A) accuracy
(B) tendency
(C) observation
(D) recognition
答案▼
(C) observation — 「觀察」野外動物行為，符合科學語境。
5. After completing all safety checks, the construction team received permission to _____ with the building project.
(A) recruit
(B) illustrate
(C) impact
(D) proceed
答案▼
(D) proceed — proceed with 表示「繼續進行某事」。

文法重點
重點一：despite / without / in spite of 後接名詞或動名詞

despite 和 in spite of 意思相同，均表示「儘管；雖然」，後面必須接名詞、代名詞或動名詞（V-ing），不可直接接子句。若後接子句，應改用 although / even though。

Despite the rain, they continued the outdoor event. 儘管下雨，他們還是繼續戶外活動。
In spite of being tired, she finished the report. 儘管疲憊，她還是完成了報告。
Without proper sleep, you cannot focus well. 沒有充足的睡眠，你無法好好專注。

重點二：分詞片語（Participial Phrase）的用法

分詞片語由現在分詞（V-ing）或過去分詞（p.p.）引導，修飾主要子句的主詞。分詞片語的主詞必須與主要子句的主詞一致，否則稱為「懸垂分詞」（dangling participle）。

Feeling nervous, she took a deep breath before walking on stage. （她感到緊張，）她上台前深吸了一口氣。
Exhausted by the long journey, the travelers fell asleep immediately. （被長途旅程搞得精疲力竭，）旅者們立刻睡著了。
Written in simple language, the book appeals to young readers. 這本書以簡單的語言寫成，吸引年輕讀者。

重點三：複合形容詞（Compound Adjectives）的構成與連字號

複合形容詞通常由兩個或多個詞以連字號（hyphen）連接，用於名詞前修飾。常見構成方式：數字 + 名詞、形容詞 + 名詞 + ed、形容詞/副詞 + 分詞。

a five-year-old child 一個五歲的孩子（數字 + 單位 + old）
a kind-hearted volunteer 一位善心的義工（形容詞 + 名詞 + ed）
a well-known scientist 一位知名科學家（副詞 + 過去分詞）
a heart-wrenching story 一個令人心碎的故事（名詞 + 現在分詞）

閱讀測驗

Human beings are social creatures by nature. The way we relate to one another — through compassion, empathy, and loyalty — shapes who we are and how we live together. Psychologists have found that people who show greater sympathy and genuine care for others tend to lead happier and more fulfilling lives.

However, not all social behavior is positive. Aggressive individuals, who prioritize winning over cooperation, can create toxic environments both at home and in the workplace. When people feel isolated from their peers, their mental health can suffer greatly. The consequences of chronic loneliness are as damaging as smoking or physical inactivity, according to recent research.

Communities also face broader challenges. In some regions, smugglers continue to violate national laws by trafficking illegal goods and people across borders. These criminals exploit innocent victims, who are often powerless to resist. It is the responsibility of governments to seize every opportunity to strengthen law enforcement and protect vulnerable populations.

On a more personal level, being faithful to one's values even under pressure is a sign of strong moral character. A generous spirit and the willingness to sacrifice short-term convenience for long-term good are qualities that loyal friends and admirable leaders share. Life is not stationary — it asks us to overcome fears, embrace change, and care for the world around us with open hearts.

(C) overcome — 克服重重困難（极寒和缺糧）才到達南極。

文法重點
重點一：代名詞 one 和 it 的區別

it 指代前面提到的特定、同一個事物；one 指代同類中的任一個（不特定）事物。簡單說，it = 那一個；one = 某一個。

I lost my pen. I need to buy a new one. 我弄丟了我的筆，我需要再買一支。（不特定的一支）
I found a wallet, and I gave it to the police. 我找到了一個錢包，我把它交給了警察。（同一個錢包）
This laptop is too heavy. I want a lighter one. 這台筆電太重了，我想要一台比較輕的。（另一台，不特定）

重點二：比較級句型 the + 比較級...the + 比較級

The + 比較級 + S + V, the + 比較級 + S + V 表示「越…就越…」，兩個子句中的比較級形容詞/副詞必須同步，主詞不可省略（除非是慣用語）。

The harder you study, the better your results will be. 你越用功讀書，成績就越好。
The more efficient the system is, the less energy it consumes. 系統越有效率，消耗的能源就越少。
The more innovations we embrace, the faster society will progress. 我們越擁抱創新，社會進步就越快。

重點三：量詞與不可數名詞的搭配

不可數名詞（如 water、information、advice、equipment）不能直接加 a/an 或加複數 -s，需要透過量詞片語來表示數量。常見量詞：a piece of、a bit of、an amount of、a great deal of（用於不可數名詞）。

a piece of advice 一個建議；a piece of equipment 一件設備
a great deal of information 大量資訊；a bit of luck 一點運氣
注意：不說 ~~an advice~~、~~informations~~、~~equipments~~

She gave me a very useful piece of advice that changed my perspective completely.
她給了我一個非常有用的建議，完全改變了我的看法。

(D) exceed

(A) identify
(B) detect
(C) distribute
(D) transform
答案▼
(B) detect — 偵測器「偵測到」以往看不見的污染物微量。
3. The country's rapid industrial growth _____ its carbon emission targets, forcing the government to revise its climate policy.
(A) enhanced
(B) launched
(C) exceeded
(D) maintained
答案▼
(C) exceeded — 工業增長「超過」了碳排放目標，exceed 表示超越預定限度。
4. Digital tools have _____ the learning experience by allowing students to access materials anytime and anywhere.
(A) sustained
(B) enhanced
(C) isolated
(D) absorbed
答案▼
(B) enhanced — 數位工具「提升了」學習體驗，enhance 表示使某事物更好。

(A) identified
(B) transformed
(C) maintained
(D) distributed

(D) distributed — 救援人員「分發」緊急物資給災難受難者。
