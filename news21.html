過去式 vs. 過去完成式 · Tense in Context

1

📖 單字介紹 Vocabulary


inventor
n. 名詞
發明家
a person who creates or designs something new
Ex: Edison was a brilliant inventor.
practical
adj. 形容詞
實用的；可行的
useful and workable in real life
Ex: We need a practical solution to this problem.
reliable
adj. 形容詞
可靠的；穩定的
consistently good and trustworthy
Ex: She is a very reliable person.
develop
v. 動詞
研發；開發
to create or improve something over time
Ex: Scientists are developing new medicines.
source
n. 名詞
來源；源頭
the origin or supply of something
Ex: The sun is a source of light and heat.
nighttime
n. 名詞
夜晚；晚間
the period of darkness between sunset and sunrise
Ex: Nighttime activities became easier with electricity.
invention
n. 名詞
發明；發明物
something new that someone has created
Ex: The telephone was Bell's greatest invention.
2

📰 文章閱讀 Reading Passage


Thomas Edison, one of history's greatest inventors, developed the first practical light bulb in 1879. Before Edison found a way to make light bulbs last longer, many scientists had tried and failed to create a reliable electric light source. His invention changed the world and made nighttime activities much easier for everyone.

⚡ 黃色標示 = 本課重要單字｜紅色框內 = 答案關鍵字 "found"（過去式）

🔑 文法重點：Before + 過去式 的時態規則

本題的關鍵在於 before 引導的時間副詞子句。
當主要子句的動詞（had tried and failed）已是過去完成式，before 子句中通常用簡單過去式，表示兩件過去的事中，「時間上稍晚發生」的那件事。

📌 規則：Before + S + V（過去式），S + had + p.p.（過去完成式）
➡ 答案選 (B) found

✗ (A) finds → 現在式，不符合過去情境
✗ (C) has found → 現在完成式，不符合過去情境
✗ (D) was finding → 過去進行式，強調「當時正在進行」，與句意不符

練習 1： Before she _____ the answer, the teacher had already explained the rule three times.

"Before Edison has found a way, many had failed."
