The Secret Geometry of Honeycombs

蜂巢背後的幾何祕密
林威老師與 AI agent 團隊製作
英文文章原文
Honeycombs are hexagonal because this shape lets bees store the most honey with the least wax, while keeping the structure strong and stable. In mathematics, this idea is captured by the “honeycomb conjecture,” which proves that if you divide a flat surface into equal areas, a pattern of regular hexagons uses the shortest total wall length, so it saves the most material. Only triangles, squares, and hexagons can completely cover a surface without gaps, but among these, hexagons enclose the largest area for the same perimeter, making them the most efficient shape for bees.

From an evolutionary biology point of view, bees that built more efficient combs could store more food and raise more young using less wax, which is very costly to produce. Colonies with slightly better comb designs would survive tough times, like cold winters or food shortages, more easily, so over millions of years natural selection favored bees whose building behavior produced hexagonal patterns. Interestingly, studies show that bees often start with soft, roughly circular wax cells, and then physical forces such as surface tension and the pressure from neighboring cells gradually pull them into neat hexagons.

文法解析
【引句 Quote】
Only triangles, squares, and hexagons can completely cover a surface without gaps...
【句型】
S + V + ... + without + N. / V-ing
【核心說明】
without 作為介係詞，表示「沒有」或「在不...的情況下」。它能引導出動作發生的伴隨狀態或先決條件。
【使用規則】
  • 後接名詞 (N.)：表示缺乏某物或某狀態。
  • 後接動名詞 (V-ing)：表示「未進行某動作而...」。
  • 用於句尾表示修飾，或句首表示強調。
【主題例句】
Bees can survive the winter without much trouble if they have enough honey.
(如果蜜蜂有足夠的蜂蜜，就能沒什麼困難地度過冬天。)
【學習提醒】
注意 without 是介係詞，後面絕對不可接原形動詞。若要表示「沒有做某事」，必須使用 V-ing。
【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】
✗ He left the room without say anything.
✓ He left the room without saying anything. (介係詞後加 V-ing)
題：She finished the exam _____ any mistakes. (A) with (B) without (C) not (D) no
答：(B)
【引句 Quote】
...hexagons enclose the largest area for the same perimeter, making them the most efficient shape for bees.
【句型】
S + V + ..., V-ing... (表結果)
【核心說明】
這是一種分詞構句，用來表示「隨之而來的結果」。當後句的主詞就是前句整件事或前句主詞，且兩者為因果關係時，可將後句動詞改為 V-ing。
【使用規則】
  • 通常放在主句之後，中間用逗號隔開。
  • V-ing 引導的片段代表一種必然、自然的結果。
  • 常可用「, which + V...」改寫。
【主題例句】
The bees used less wax, saving a lot of energy for the whole colony.
(蜜蜂使用較少的蠟，為整個群落節省了大量能量。)
【學習提醒】
這種 V-ing 結構與前面的句子具有明確的因果或連續關係。如果是「被動」產生的結果，則改用 V-p.p.，但此用法以主動居多。
【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】
✗ The snow melted, made the road slippery.
✓ The snow melted, making the road slippery. (分詞構句表結果)
題：He forgot his umbrella, _____ him get wet in the rain. (A) caused (B) causing (C) to cause (D) and cause
答：(B)
【引句 Quote】
...natural selection favored bees whose building behavior produced hexagonal patterns.
【句型】
N. (先行詞) + whose + N. + V / S + V
【核心說明】
whose 是關係代名詞的所有格，用來表示先行詞與後面名詞之間的「所有關係」（某人的某物/某事）。
【使用規則】
  • 先行詞可以是人，也可以是事物。
  • whose 後面必須緊接一個名詞。
  • 其引導的形容詞子句不可有冠詞 (the/a) 修飾該名詞。
【主題例句】
Mathematics is a subject whose rules apply to everything in nature.
(數學是一門其規則適用於自然界萬物的學科。)
【學習提醒】
注意 dont use "of which the..." if possible, "whose" is more modern and efficient for both people and things.
【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】
✗ I know the girl who's cat is missing. (who's = who is)
✓ I know the girl whose cat is missing. (所有格用 whose)
題：The mountain, _____ peak is covered with snow, is very high. (A) who (B) that (C) whose (D) which
答：(C)

練文筆食材
① Among these... (在這些...之中)
→ 適用：在多個項目中指出特定一個的優點或特質。
例：Among these methods, the hexagonal pattern is the most efficient.

② In this way... (以此方式...)
→ 適用：總結前述動作帶來的影響。
例：In this way, nature achieves optimal design without planning.

③ Not only... but also... (不只...而且...)
→ 適用：強調事物的雙重優點。
例：The structure is not only material-saving but also incredibly strong.

④ ...making them the most... (...使得它們成為最...)
→ 適用：說明前述事實導致的最終結果。
例：Hexagons enclose the largest area, making them the most efficient shape.

⑤ ...would survive..., more easily (能更輕易地生存)
→ 適用：描述在特定條件下具有的優勢。
例：Colonies with better designs would survive tough times more easily.
