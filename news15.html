【區塊一】英文文章原文

英文文章原文 Original Article

Coming out of their shell, conquering land and sea. If all turtles were alike, they would not be able to survive in deserts, ponds, and oceans at the same time. Yet they do. This ability to thrive in all these environments comes from the wide differences among turtle species. Based on their habitats, turtles are usually divided into three types. Land-dwelling tortoises, semi-aquatic freshwater turtles, and fully marine sea turtles.

Tortoises are the best example of adaptation to life on land. Equipped with strong, stout legs that resemble small elephant feet, they can walk across rough terrain and dig burrows for shelter. Their shells are typically high and dome-shaped, which provides extra space for their limbs and protection from predators.

Freshwater turtles are well-suited to life both on land and in water. Their webbed feet help them swim efficiently. While their flexible necks and bodies allow them to maintain stability in moving water. In addition, their flat, smooth shells help them cut through water with minimal drag and without being easily seen. Sea turtles are built for life in the ocean. Their legs have evolved into flat, paddle-shaped flippers that support long-distance swimming. Streamlined bodies and shells further reduce water resistance. Although sea turtles spend most of their lives underwater, they are reptiles and must breathe air through their lungs. They can hold their breath for a long time, especially while resting underwater, but they still need to surface to breathe. Another notable feature of sea turtles is that their sex is determined by the temperature of the sand where their eggs are buried. Warmer sand tends to produce females, while cooler sand often produces males.

Coming out of their shell, conquering land and sea. Turtles are full of surprises that go beyond their slow and steady reputation. One of the most remarkable features of a turtle is its shell. Unlike the shells of crabs or snails, a turtle shell is part of its skeleton. Since nerves run through the shell, A turtle can actually feel when it is touched. So petting a turtle shell isn't like patting a hard, lifeless shield. Instead, it's more like touching part of its body.

Turtles are also known for their longevity. However, not all turtles are cut from the same cloth. In general, land-dwelling tortoises tend to outlive sea turtles. As reptiles, most turtles have a slow metabolism, which means they burn energy slowly. Their tough shells also help them survive in the wild by reducing the risk of predation. Feeding mostly on plants or small insects, they are free from the physical stress that comes with hunting. With lower energy demands, fewer dangers, and a relatively calm lifestyle, turtles are well-equipped to live long lives.

Since 2000, people around the globe have been celebrating World Turtle Day on May 23rd. This annual event reminds people to keep an eye out for the threats these creatures face, such as plastic pollution, climate change, and habitat loss. By reducing plastic waste, supporting conservation efforts, and protecting their homes, we can help ensure these amazing animals continue their long journey on Earth.

八句精讀 8 Key Sentence Analyses

①句子一

If all turtles were alike, they would not be able to survive in deserts, ponds, and oceans at the same time.

如果所有烏龜都一樣，牠們就無法同時在沙漠、池塘和海洋中生存。

文法解析：這句是第二類條件句，句型為 If + S + 過去式, S + would + 原形動詞。were 並不是在說過去，而是用來表示與現在事實相反的假設。主要子句中的 would not be able to 表示「將無法……」，強調如果缺乏差異，生存範圍就會受限。

alike：相似的survive：生存at the same time：同時

②句子二

This ability to thrive in all these environments comes from the wide differences among turtle species.

這種能在所有這些環境中茁壯生存的能力，來自烏龜物種之間的巨大差異。

文法解析：This ability 是主詞，comes from 是主要動詞片語。to thrive in all these environments 是不定詞片語，修飾 ability，說明是哪一種能力；這裡的 to 是不定詞，不是介係詞。among turtle species 表示「在烏龜物種之間」，通常用 among 指三者以上的群體。

thrive：茁壯成長environments：環境species：物種

③句子三

Equipped with strong, stout legs that resemble small elephant feet, they can walk across rough terrain and dig burrows for shelter.

牠們配備強壯、粗短且像小象腳的腿，因此能走過崎嶇地形並挖洞穴作為庇護。

文法解析：Equipped with... 是過去分詞構句，補充說明主詞 they 的身體特徵。that resemble small elephant feet 是形容詞子句，修飾 legs。walk across rough terrain 與 dig burrows 是兩個並列動作，說明這些腿帶來的功能。

equipped with：配備有resemble：像burrows：洞穴

④句子四

In addition, their flat, smooth shells help them cut through water with minimal drag and without being easily seen.

此外，牠們扁平光滑的殼能幫助牠們以最小阻力切開水流，且不容易被看見。

文法解析：help + 受詞 + 原形動詞是常見句型，因此 help them cut 不需要加 to。with minimal drag 表示「以最小阻力」，without being easily seen 是介系詞 without 後接動名詞被動式。being seen 表示「被看見」，修飾牠們在水中的隱蔽效果。

minimal drag：最小阻力cut through water：切開水流without being seen：不被看見

⑤句子五

Although sea turtles spend most of their lives underwater, they are reptiles and must breathe air through their lungs.

雖然海龜一生大多數時間都在水下度過，但牠們是爬蟲類，必須透過肺呼吸空氣。

文法解析：Although 引導讓步副詞子句，表示「雖然……」。主要子句 they are reptiles and must breathe air through their lungs 才是句子的重點。must 表示生理上的必要性，through their lungs 則用 through 表示空氣通過肺部這個途徑。

although：雖然reptiles：爬蟲類through their lungs：透過肺部

⑥句子六

Another notable feature of sea turtles is that their sex is determined by the temperature of the sand where their eggs are buried.

海龜另一個值得注意的特徵是，牠們的性別由卵被埋藏處沙子的溫度決定。

文法解析：that their sex is determined... 是名詞子句，放在 be 動詞後作主詞補語，說明 feature 的內容。is determined by 是被動語態，表示「由……決定」。where their eggs are buried 是形容詞子句，修飾 sand，說明是哪裡的沙子。

notable feature：值得注意的特徵is determined by：由……決定where：在……的地方

⑦句子七

Since nerves run through the shell, A turtle can actually feel when it is touched.

因為神經穿過龜殼，所以烏龜在殼被碰到時其實能感覺得到。

文法解析：Since 在此表示原因，等於 because。run through the shell 中的 through 表示穿過或貫穿。when it is touched 是時間副詞子句，也含有被動語態 is touched，表示「當牠被碰觸時」。

nerves：神經run through：貫穿actually：實際上

⑧句子八

Feeding mostly on plants or small insects, they are free from the physical stress that comes with hunting.

牠們主要以植物或小昆蟲為食，因此不用承受伴隨狩獵而來的身體壓力。

文法解析：Feeding mostly on... 是現在分詞構句，表示原因或背景條件。be free from 表示「不受……影響；免於……」。that comes with hunting 是形容詞子句，修飾 physical stress，說明這種壓力來自狩獵活動。

feed on：以……為食be free from：免於physical stress：身體壓力

【區塊四】文法解析

文法解析 Grammar Focus

【第二類條件句】Second Conditional 與現在事實相反的假設

【引句 Quote】

If all turtles were alike, they would not be able to survive in deserts, ponds, and oceans at the same time.

【句型】

If + S + V-ed / were, S + would + V

【核心說明】

第二類條件句用來描述與現在事實相反，或不太可能發生的假設。本文實際上要表達的是烏龜並不都一樣，因此牠們才能適應不同環境。

【使用規則】

  • If 子句用過去式，但意思不一定是過去。
  • be 動詞常用 were，尤其在正式寫作中。
  • 主要子句常用 would、could 或 might 加原形動詞。
  • 否定句可用 would not / could not 表示假設結果不成立。

【主題例句】

If tortoises had thin shells, they would be less protected from predators.
（如果陸龜的殼很薄，牠們就比較不受保護，容易遭捕食者攻擊。）

【學習提醒】

這種句型的重點不是時間，而是「假設性」。不要看到 were 就直接翻成過去，要根據 if 與 would 判斷語氣。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ If all turtles are alike, they would not survive in many places.

✓ If all turtles were alike, they would not survive in many places.（與現在事實相反的假設，if 子句要用過去式。）

試題：If sea turtles ________ lungs, they would not need to surface for air.
A. do not have B. did not have C. will not have D. are not having

答案：B。說明：這是與事實相反的假設，if 子句使用過去式 did not have。

【分詞構句】Participial Construction 分詞構句

【引句 Quote】

Equipped with strong, stout legs that resemble small elephant feet, they can walk across rough terrain and dig burrows for shelter.

【句型】

V-ing / V-ed + ..., S + V

【核心說明】

分詞構句可以用來補充原因、背景、時間或附帶狀況，使句子更緊湊。Equipped with... 是過去分詞開頭，表示主詞 they 具有某種被賦予或配備的特徵。

【使用規則】

  • 主詞與動作是主動關係時，常用 V-ing。
  • 主詞與動作是被動關係時，常用 V-ed。
  • 分詞構句的邏輯主詞通常要和主要子句主詞一致。
  • 分詞片語放句首時，後面通常加逗號。

【主題例句】

Protected by a hard shell, the turtle can hide from many predators.
（受到堅硬外殼保護，烏龜能躲避許多捕食者。）

【學習提醒】

寫分詞構句時最常見的問題是主詞不一致。分詞片語描述的對象必須能清楚對應到主要子句的主詞。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ Equipped with strong legs, rough terrain is easy for tortoises.

✓ Equipped with strong legs, tortoises can cross rough terrain.（配備強壯腿的是 tortoises，不是 terrain。）

試題：______ mostly on plants, tortoises avoid much of the stress of hunting.
A. Feed B. Feeding C. Fed D. To feed

答案：B。說明：主詞 tortoises 主動進食，句首用現在分詞 Feeding。

【讓步副詞子句】Concessive Clause although 讓步句

【引句 Quote】

Although sea turtles spend most of their lives underwater, they are reptiles and must breathe air through their lungs.

【句型】

Although + S + V, S + V

【核心說明】

Although 引導讓步子句，表示「雖然……」，用來承認一個看似會影響結果的事實。本文承認海龜長時間在水下生活，但主要重點是牠們仍是爬蟲類，必須呼吸空氣。

【使用規則】

  • Although 子句放句首時，後面通常加逗號。
  • Although 與 but 通常不在同一句中同時使用。
  • 主要子句才是作者要強調的核心訊息。
  • though 比 although 較口語，也可放句尾。

【主題例句】

Although turtles move slowly, they have survived on Earth for a very long time.
（雖然烏龜移動緩慢，牠們已在地球上生存很長一段時間。）

【學習提醒】

讓步句適合用在自然科學與社會議題寫作中，因為它能同時呈現表面現象與真正重點。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ Although sea turtles live underwater, but they must breathe air.

✓ Although sea turtles live underwater, they must breathe air.（Although 已有「雖然」意思，不需再加 but。）

試題：______ tortoises live on land, they still belong to the larger turtle group.
A. Because B. Although C. So D. Unless

答案：B。說明：前後形成讓步對比，應選 Although。

折疊四：練文筆食材⌄

① If + S + were/V-ed, S + would + V（如果……就會……）
→ 適用：提出與事實相反的假設。
例：If all animals had the same body shape, they would not survive in different habitats.

② This ability to + V comes from...（這種……的能力來自……）
→ 適用：說明能力或特徵的原因。
例：This ability to adapt comes from millions of years of evolution.

③ Based on + N, S + V（根據……，……）
→ 適用：分類、分析或下結論。
例：Based on their habitats, animals can be divided into several groups.

④ Equipped with + N, S + V（配備有……，……）
→ 適用：描述生物或工具的特徵與功能。
例：Equipped with sharp claws, the animal can climb trees easily.

⑤ help + O + V（幫助某人／某物做……）
→ 適用：說明功能或效果。
例：Webbed feet help turtles swim efficiently.

⑥ with minimal + N（以最少的……）
→ 適用：說明效率或阻力低。
例：The new design moves through air with minimal resistance.

⑦ Although + S + V, S + V（雖然……，……）
→ 適用：呈現轉折或讓步。
例：Although sea turtles live underwater, they must breathe air.

⑧ be determined by + N（由……決定）
→ 適用：說明因果或控制因素。
例：The result is determined by temperature and time.

⑨ Unlike + N, S + V（不像……，……）
→ 適用：比較兩種事物的差異。
例：Unlike fish, turtles need to surface for air.

⑩ Since + S + V, S + V（因為……，……）
→ 適用：說明原因。
例：Since nerves run through the shell, turtles can feel touch.

⑪ be known for + N（以……聞名）
→ 適用：介紹特色或名聲。
例：Turtles are known for their longevity.

⑫ Feeding mostly on + N, S + V（主要以……為食，……）
→ 適用：說明飲食習性與結果。
例：Feeding mostly on plants, the animal uses less energy than active hunters.

⑬ be free from + N（免於……）
→ 適用：表示不受某事影響。
例：A calm lifestyle can keep animals free from unnecessary stress.

⑭ By + V-ing, S + can + V（藉由……，……可以……）
→ 適用：提出解決方法。
例：By reducing plastic waste, we can protect marine animals.
