原文 Original Article

The Amazing Life of Swifts Nature is full of incredible creatures, and the small dark birds known as swifts are certainly no exception. As a matter of fact, swifts are some of the best flyers in the animal world. Although they look similar to swallows, swifts actually belong to a different bird family and are more closely related to hummingbirds. While most birds spend a good part of their day standing on branches or walking on the ground, swifts have taken flight to a completely new level. Their long, narrow wings and streamlined bodies are perfectly shaped for a life in the air. In fact, these remarkable creatures have adapted almost every aspect of their lives to flight. There are around 100 swift species worldwide, but the common swift is one of the most studied. Scientists estimate that common swifts can stay airborne for up to 10 months continuously. They have a lot of wildlife in the air, and even mate on the wing. Because they have tiny feet and short legs, swifts cannot walk or perch like most birds. When they do land, they usually hold on to vertical surfaces such as cliffs or walls.

One of the most amazing things about common swifts is their speed and endurance. During its lifetime, a common swift may travel millions of kilometers, equal to several trips to the moon and back. Every year, these birds migrate from Europe and Asia to Africa and take advantage of strong winds along the way. These long-distance journeys are among nature's greatest wonders. Young common swifts are also full of surprises. When nesting, the parents may leave their chicks alone for days if the weather turns bad. The baby birds survive by entering a state called torpor that slows their heart rate and helps them save energy. When the chicks are ready to fly, they launch into the sky and begin their travels across different continents. Although swifts are common in many parts of the world, their numbers are decreasing in some regions because of habitat loss. Although swifts in Europe often nest are being replaced by modern ones without the small gaps they need. Thankfully, many people are now building special nest boxes to help them. With a little help from us, these wonderful birds can keep filling our skies.

①複合句與同位概念

Although they look similar to swallows, swifts actually belong to a different bird family and are more closely related to hummingbirds.

雖然牠們看起來和燕子相似，雨燕其實屬於不同的鳥類家族，並且和蜂鳥的關係更接近。

文法解析：Although 引導讓步副詞子句，表示「雖然……但是……」。主要子句有兩個並列動詞片語：belong to 與 are related to。英文中 although 不與 but 同時使用，中文翻譯可自然譯出轉折感。

②While 對比句

③被動與目的

牠們細長的翅膀與流線型身體，完美地適合空中生活。

文法解析

Grammar Focus

1. 讓步副詞子句：Although

【引句 Quote】
Although they look similar to swallows, swifts actually belong to a different bird family.

【句型】
Although + S + V, S + V...

【核心說明】
Although 引導讓步副詞子句，用來表達「雖然 A，但是 B 仍然成立」。這種句型適合用來呈現外表與事實、期待與結果之間的落差。

【使用規則】

  • Although 放句首時，子句後通常加逗號。
  • Although 不可和 but 同時連接同一組主從句。
  • Though 語氣較口語；although 較正式。
  • Even though 比 although 語氣更強。

【主題例句】
Although swifts are small, they can travel across continents.
（雖然雨燕體型小，牠們卻能橫跨不同大洲旅行。）

【學習提醒】
中文常說「雖然……但是……」，但英文只需要 although 或 but 擇一使用。寫作時若句首已用 Although，主要子句不要再加 but。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】
✗ Although swifts are small, but they fly very far.
✓ Although swifts are small, they fly very far.（although 與 but 不並用）

  1. 改錯：Although the weather was bad, but the chicks survived.
  2. 選擇：______ swifts look like swallows, they belong to a different bird family. (A) Although (B) Because

2. 名詞子句：that + S + V

【引句 Quote】
Scientists estimate that common swifts can stay airborne for up to 10 months continuously.

【句型】
S + V + that + S + V...

【核心說明】
that 子句可作動詞的受詞，表示「……這件事」。在本文中，that 後面的完整句子是科學家估計的內容。

【使用規則】

  • that 子句必須有主詞與動詞。
  • 常搭配 think, believe, know, estimate, suggest 等動詞。
  • 口語中 that 有時可省略，但正式寫作保留較清楚。
  • 主句與子句時態要依語意判斷。

【主題例句】
Researchers believe that swifts sleep while flying.
（研究人員相信雨燕會在飛行時睡覺。）

【學習提醒】
that 在名詞子句中只負責引出內容，不等於關係代名詞 that。判斷方法是看 that 後面是否是一個完整句子，且整個子句是否作前面動詞的受詞。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】
✗ Scientists estimate common swifts can stay airborne up to 10 months continuously in very formal writing.
✓ Scientists estimate that common swifts can stay airborne for up to 10 months continuously.（正式寫作保留 that，時間長度用 for）

  1. 合併：Scientists believe. Swifts can remain in the air for months.
  2. 選擇：Experts suggest ______ habitat loss is a serious problem. (A) that (B) what

3. 分詞構句：When + V-ing

【引句 Quote】
When nesting, the parents may leave their chicks alone for days if the weather turns bad.

【句型】
When + V-ing, S + V...

【核心說明】
When nesting 是 When they are nesting 的化簡。當副詞子句的主詞與主要子句主詞相同，且語意清楚時，可以刪除主詞並把動詞改成現在分詞。

【使用規則】

  • 化簡前後的主詞通常要一致。
  • 主動語意用 V-ing；被動語意常用過去分詞。
  • when 可保留，讓時間關係更清楚。
  • 若主詞不同，避免隨意化簡，以免語意錯亂。

【主題例句】
When crossing continents, swifts depend on strong winds.
（橫跨大洲時，雨燕依賴強風。）

【學習提醒】
分詞構句不是單純把任何句子縮短。先確認主詞是否一致，再決定是否能化簡；否則讀者可能誤會動作是由主要子句主詞執行。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】
✗ When nesting, the weather may turn bad.（變成 weather 在築巢）
✓ When the swifts are nesting, the weather may turn bad.（主詞不同，不宜化簡）

  1. 化簡：When they migrate to Africa, swifts use strong winds.
  2. 改錯：When flying at night, the moon guided the birds.

練文筆食材⌄

① be full of + N（充滿……）
→ 適用：描述自然、城市、人生經驗。
例：Nature is full of lessons that humans can learn from.

② be known as + N（被稱為……）
→ 適用：介紹人物、動物或地方。
例：The bird is known as one of the best flyers in nature.

③ no exception（也不例外）
→ 適用：把主題接到前一句的通則。
例：Many animals face danger, and swifts are no exception.

④ Although + S + V, S + V（雖然……但是……）
→ 適用：表達外表與事實的反差。
例：Although the bird is small, it can travel across continents.

⑤ While + S + V, S + V（然而；相較之下）
→ 適用：比較兩種不同情況。
例：While most birds rest on branches, swifts spend much of life in the air.

⑥ be perfectly shaped for + N（非常適合……）
→ 適用：描述設計、身體構造或功能。
例：Their wings are perfectly shaped for long-distance flight.

⑦ adapt A to B（使 A 適應 B）
→ 適用：寫環境、生物或科技變化。
例：Many species adapt their behavior to a changing climate.

⑧ estimate that + S + V（估計……）
→ 適用：引用研究或數據。
例：Scientists estimate that the population is falling in some regions.

⑨ for up to + 時間/數量（長達；高達）
→ 適用：強調範圍上限。
例：Some swifts may remain airborne for up to ten months.

⑩ Because + S + V, S + V（因為……所以……）
→ 適用：清楚說明原因。
例：Because swifts have tiny feet, they rarely walk on the ground.

⑪ such as + N（例如……）
→ 適用：補充具體例子。
例：They hold on to vertical surfaces such as cliffs or walls.

⑫ take advantage of + N（利用……）
→ 適用：描述善用資源或條件。
例：Migrating birds take advantage of strong winds.

⑬ by + V-ing（藉由……）
→ 適用：說明方法。
例：The chicks save energy by entering a state of torpor.

⑭ because of + N（因為……）
→ 適用：用名詞片語表原因。
例：Their numbers are decreasing because of habitat loss.

⑮ With a little help from us, S + can + V（只要我們稍加幫助，……）
→ 適用：結尾提出希望或行動。
例：With a little help from us, wild birds can keep filling our skies.
