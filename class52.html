📖 今日 5 個單字
routine/ruːˈtiːn/n./adj.
日常慣例；例行的
She follows a strict morning routine every day before school.
chores/tʃɔːrz/n.
家務事；雜務
Doing household chores teaches children responsibility and teamwork.

📌 小提示：efficient（有效率）vs effective（有效果）— efficient 強調節省時間/資源，effective 強調達成目標。

📝 文法改錯日
Week 1 · 日常生活類 · 15 分鐘
1. She has a daily routine of wake up at 6 a.m.
查看答案 ▼
wake → waking
介系詞 of 後接動名詞。✏️ …a daily routine of waking up at 6 a.m.
2. Doing house chores are tiring but necessary.
查看答案 ▼
are → is
動名詞片語作主詞，動詞用單數。✏️ Doing house chores is tiring…
3. It is importants to set priorities before starting work.
查看答案 ▼
importants → important
形容詞不加 -s。✏️ It is important to set priorities…
4. She scheduled time to discuss the budget of next month.
查看答案 ▼
✅ 這句正確！
discuss 為及物動詞，不需 about；budget of next month 用法正確。
🌟 本日句型重點
make + N + a priority（把…列為優先事項）
Make sleep a priority if you want to stay healthy and focused.
She made exercise a daily priority during summer vacation.

📝 文法改錯日

1. She concentrates in her studies every evening.
查看答案 ▼
in → on
concentrate on = 專注於。✏️ She concentrates on her studies…
2. They collaborated each other on the science project.
查看答案 ▼
each other → with each other
collaborate with = 與…合作。✏️ They collaborated with each other…

查看答案 ▼
careful → carefully
修飾動詞需用副詞。✏️ …evaluated the students' work carefully.
4. Academic performance are important for getting into a good university.
查看答案 ▼
are → is
Academic performance 為不可數名詞，動詞用單數。

rather than + V-ing（而不是…）
Students should collaborate rather than competing against each other.

🔧 文法重點：被動語態 (Passive Voice)
主動： People celebrate this tradition every year.
被動： This tradition is celebrated every year.

結構： be動詞 + 過去分詞 (V-ed/p.p.)
表示時間： 現在式用 is/are，過去式用 was/were

Week 7 · 比較級與最高級 · 20 分鐘
🔧 文法重點：比較級與最高級
比較級： adj. + -er / more + adj. → She runs faster than me.
最高級： the + adj. + -est / the most + adj. → He is the most competitive player.

注意：兩音節以上的形容詞通常用 more/most（如 competitive, motivated）
🔍 找出並改正錯誤

1. She is more taller than her sister.

查看答案 ▼
✅ She is taller than her sister. （不能同時用 more 和 -er）

2. He is the most fast runner on the team.

查看答案 ▼
✅ He is the fastest runner on the team. （fast 是單音節，用 -est）

3. This exercise requires more coordination as that one.

查看答案 ▼
✅ This exercise requires more coordination than that one. （比較級後用 than，不用 as）

4. Her endurance is the most strong of all athletes.

查看答案 ▼
✅ Her endurance is the strongest of all athletes. （strong 是單音節形容詞，用 -est）

✏️ 文法改錯日
Week 8 · 連接詞與轉折語 · 20 分鐘
🔧 文法重點：連接詞與轉折語
對比： although / even though / however / despite
例： Although she felt anxious, she gave the speech bravely.
注意： despite 後接名詞/V-ing，不接子句（despite the fact that... ✅）
例： Despite her anxiety, she performed well. ✅
錯誤： Despite she was anxious, she performed well. ❌
🔍 找出並改正錯誤

1. Despite she felt nervous, she overcame her fear.

查看答案 ▼
✅ Although/Even though she felt nervous, she overcame her fear. （despite 後不接子句）

2. He has a good mindset, however he still struggles with anxiety.

查看答案 ▼
✅ He has a good mindset; however, he still struggles with anxiety. （however 連接兩個獨立句，前面要用分號或句號）

3. Although building resilience is hard, but it is worth it.

查看答案 ▼
✅ Although building resilience is hard, it is worth it. （although 和 but 不能同時使用，去掉 but）
