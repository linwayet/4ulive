📐 Part 2 ｜ 三大文法重點（璇 115 學測錯題分析）▼

璇詞彙題很穩，主要弱點在綜合測驗（6/10）和混合題（2/4）。今天聚焦三大常錯題型。

① 邏輯連接詞辨析　It turns out / All in all / In other words / What is more

★ 來源：115 學測 Q11（璇選 D「all in all」，正解 B「it turns out」）

It turns out (that) ...　＝　結果發現⋯（引出意外或最後揭曉的結論）
All in all　＝　總結來說（用於整體歸納）
In other words　＝　換句話說（同義改述）
What is more　＝　更甚者（補充更強的點）

辨識訣竅：看後面是「意外的事實」用 it turns out；看後面是「整體結論」用 all in all。

White rhinoceroses do the same thing. It turns out their meeting place is a giant pile of poop.
白犀牛也做一樣的事。結果發現，牠們聚會的地方是一大堆糞便。（115 Q11 原句）
She studied for hours. It turns out the test was much easier than expected.
她讀了好幾個小時。結果發現那場考試比預期簡單很多。
All in all, the trip to the medieval castle was the highlight of our summer.
總結來說，那趟中世紀城堡之旅是我們夏天最棒的回憶。

② 完整片語的「動詞 + 介系詞」搭配　point to / take precedence over / one of a kind

★ 來源：115 學測 Q26（璇選 H「minimal」，正解 J「point to」）；Q50（璇寫「one」，正解「one of a kind」）

point to + N　＝　指向；建議；強調（動詞片語，非形容詞）
take precedence over + N　＝　優先於⋯
one of a kind　＝　獨一無二的（必須完整三字）
add fuel to the fire　＝　火上加油（比喻）

注意：「point to」是動詞片語、「minimal」是形容詞 ─ 兩者詞性不同。看上下文要先判斷需要的是「動詞」還是「形容詞」。

Some studies recommend napping for 20–40 minutes, while others point to shorter naps.
有些研究建議午睡 20–40 分鐘，而其他研究則指向更短的午睡。（115 Q26 原句）
In some cultures, individual rights take precedence over the needs of the group.
在某些文化中，個人權利優先於群體需求。
This handmade castle model is truly one of a kind.
這個手工城堡模型真的是獨一無二的。（115 Q50 完整片語）

③ be + 過去分詞　被動結構（含 be meant to be / be blended with）

★ 來源：115 學測 Q18（璇選 B「brought up as」，正解 D「meant to be」）；Q48（璇寫「product」，正解「blended」）

S + be + 過去分詞 (Vp.p.) + 介系詞　＝　被動句式
be meant to be + N　＝　本意是；被設計成
be blended with + N　＝　與⋯融合
be designed to + 原形 V　＝　被設計來做⋯
be intended for + N　＝　為⋯而設計
Dim sum is meant to be an enjoyable dining experience.
點心本來就是一種愉快的用餐體驗。（115 Q18 原句）
Local features are perfectly blended with foreign elements.
在地特色與外國元素完美融合。（115 Q48 原句，注意是 blended，不是 product）
The narrow staircase was designed to slow down attackers.
那座狹窄的樓梯被設計來拖慢攻擊者。

The Spiral Staircase: A Castle's Hidden Strategy

Many people are captivated by the spiral staircases inside medieval castles, but few realize they were not built only for beauty. They were also strategically designed for defense. In an age when stone structures protected royal families, every detail mattered.

The stairs were narrow and turned in a clockwise direction. Attackers who had to ascend these narrow steps could swing their swords only with their left hand, while right-handed defenders at the top had full freedom to attack downward. It turns out that this simple feature of design—a single curve—often made the difference between victory and defeat. The castle's strategy, in other words, was hidden in its structure.

Today these old fortresses are no longer used for war. They are meant to be visited, photographed, and protected as national monuments. Many of them are beautifully blended with the surrounding hills, attracting tourists from around the world. A magnificent staircase that once helped royalty survive is now a truly one of a kind historical experience for every visitor. Even if you have never dwelled in such a grand place, walking up its stone steps connects you to centuries of human stories.

中譯重點：許多人被中世紀城堡中的螺旋梯迷住，但很少人意識到它們不只是為了美觀，更是策略性地設計來作防禦。在那個石造建築保護王室家族的年代，每個細節都至關重要。
樓梯狹窄、順時針旋轉。被迫攀登這些狹窄階梯的攻擊者，只能用左手揮劍，而站在上方的右撇子防衛者則能自由攻擊。結果證明這個簡單的設計特色——一個彎曲——往往決定了勝負。換句話說，城堡的戰略就藏在它的結構之中。
今天這些古堡不再作戰。它們本意是讓人參觀、拍照，並作為國家古蹟受到保護。許多城堡與周圍山丘優美地融合，吸引世界各地的遊客。一座曾幫助皇室生存的壯麗樓梯，如今成為對每位訪客獨一無二的歷史體驗。即使你從未住過這麼宏偉的地方，踏上它的石階，仍能讓你連結到數世紀的人類故事。

  3. defend

  1. one of a kind
  2. under construction
  3. Grade I listed

  1. was meant to be designed
  2. was meant to design
  3. is meant to be enjoyed
  4. was designed
👆 點此查看答案 ＆ 解析▼
1. (A) strategically　策略地 — 修飾動詞 designed，需要副詞；strategically + designed 是學測螺旋梯文章原句搭配。
2. (B) prominent　顯著的 — most prominent features 是固定搭配，「最顯眼的特色」。
3. (A) captivate　吸引／使著迷 — 「millions of tourists」是受詞，captivate 與 attract 同義；defend / construct / dwell 語意不合。
4. (A) one of a kind　獨一無二的 — 完整片語 one of a kind（115 Q50 還原），三字缺一不可。
5. (D) was designed　被設計來 — 雖然 (C) is meant to be enjoyed 文法對，但語意不符（敵人不該被「享受」）；正確是「被設計來防止敵人攀登」，be designed to + V。
