Unit 1　A Big Week in Taipei

Every June, the world's biggest computer companies come to Taipei for a huge trade show called Computex. This year, the theme was "AI Together," and the leaders of NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel all gave speeches in person. Reporters from around the world filled the exhibition halls, and one product was on everyone's lips: the AI PC. For many visitors, it felt like the start of a new chapter in personal computing — the biggest change since laptops replaced desktop computers in our homes.

📌 中文摘要：每年六月的台北 Computex 是全球科技盛會，今年主題是「AI Together」，三大晶片公司執行長親自來台，焦點全在 AI 電腦上。

Unit 2　What Is an AI PC?

So what exactly is an AI PC? In simple terms, it is a computer with artificial intelligence built directly into its hardware. A traditional computer follows your commands: you click, it responds. An AI PC goes further. It can understand your voice, summarize a long document, translate a foreign-language email, or clean up a blurry family photo — all by itself, in seconds. You do not need to be a computer expert to use these features; the machine is designed to understand everyday language, just like talking to a helpful friend.

📌 中文摘要：AI 電腦把人工智慧直接內建在硬體中，能聽懂日常語言，幫你摘要文件、翻譯信件、修復照片，不必是電腦專家也能使用。
⬆ 回到單元地圖

Unit 3　The Brain Inside: the NPU

The secret of the AI PC is a small chip called the NPU, which stands for Neural Processing Unit. Think of your computer as an office. The CPU is the manager who handles general work, the GPU is the artist who draws pictures, and the NPU is the new specialist whose only job is AI. Engineers measure its power in TOPS — trillions of operations per second. Last year, a typical AI laptop offered about 40 TOPS. At Computex 2026, the newest chips reached as high as 180 TOPS. In one year, the "AI brain" inside our computers has become more than four times stronger.

📌 中文摘要：AI 電腦的核心是 NPU（神經處理器），專門負責 AI 運算；算力單位是 TOPS，從去年約 40 一口氣跳到今年最高 180，一年內強了四倍多。
⬆ 回到單元地圖

Unit 4　AI Without the Internet

Here is the part that matters most for families: the new AI PCs can run AI offline. In the past, when you asked AI a question, your words traveled to a faraway server and came back with an answer. Now, the AI lives inside your own machine. This is called on-device AI, and it brings two big benefits. First, speed — there is no waiting for the internet. Second, privacy — your photos, your health records, and your personal letters never leave your computer. For anyone who worries about where their private information goes, this is wonderful news.

📌 中文摘要：新一代 AI 電腦可以「斷網」執行 AI，資料不必送到遠端伺服器，速度更快，照片、健康紀錄等隱私也完全留在自己的電腦裡。
⬆ 回到單元地圖

Unit 5　A Battery That Lasts All Day

Another pleasant surprise from Computex 2026 is battery life. Thanks to new chip designs, some of the latest AI laptops can run for nearly twenty hours on a single charge. Imagine leaving home in the morning without a charger, using your laptop at a coffee shop, at a meeting, and on the train, and still having power left at night. The machines are also lighter than ever — one new model weighs only 1.19 kilograms, lighter than a bag of rice. For people who carry a computer between home, work, and family activities, this changes everything.

📌 中文摘要：新晶片設計讓部分 AI 筆電續航接近 20 小時、機身輕到 1.19 公斤，出門一整天不用帶充電器。

Unit 6　From Chatbot to Assistant

The biggest idea at this year's show was the shift from "generative AI" to "agentic AI." A generative AI is like a clever shop assistant who answers your questions. An AI agent is like a personal secretary who actually does the work for you. Instead of asking, "How do I plan a trip to Japan?", you can simply say, "Plan a five-day trip to Kyoto for my family," and the computer will compare flights, check hotels, and build a schedule across different apps. Industry leaders agree that the PC will become the home of these agents, because a personal task deserves a personal, private machine.

📌 中文摘要：今年最大趨勢是從「生成式 AI」走向「代理式 AI」——AI 從回答問題的店員，變成能跨軟體替你完成任務的私人秘書，而個人電腦正是這位秘書的家。
⬆ 回到單元地圖

Unit 7　Taiwan at the Center

Why do the world's most powerful tech leaders fly to Taipei every year? Because Taiwan sits at the heart of the global technology supply chain. The advanced chips are made here, and famous Taiwanese brands such as ASUS, Acer, and MSI build the laptops that carry them. At this year's show, NVIDIA and Taiwan's own MediaTek introduced a powerful new chip together, and the first laptops using it will be made by Taiwanese companies later this year. When we talk about the AI PC, we are also talking about the skill and hard work of engineers in our own backyard.

📌 中文摘要：台灣是全球科技供應鏈的中心：先進晶片在這裡製造，華碩、宏碁、微星打造筆電，輝達更與聯發科聯手推出新晶片——AI PC 的故事就是台灣的故事。

Unit 8　Should I Buy One Now?

So, should you replace your old computer today? Experts suggest asking three simple questions. First, how old is your current machine? If it is more than five or six years old, the jump in speed and battery life will amaze you. Second, what do you really need? If you only browse the web and write emails, a basic model is enough — do not pay extra for power you will never use. Third, can you wait a little? The newest AI laptops will arrive in stores in the second half of this year, and prices usually become friendlier a few months after launch. The wise shopper is patient, informed, and never afraid of new technology.

📌 中文摘要：換機前先問三件事：舊電腦幾歲了、自己真正的需求是什麼、能不能等下半年新機上市後價格回穩——聰明的消費者有耐心、有資訊、也不怕新科技。

📖 Glossary 術語表

trade show (n.) — 商展、貿易展。廠商展示新產品的大型活動，Computex 就是全球最大的電腦商展之一。
exhibition hall (n.) — 展覽館。舉辦展覽的大型場館，例如台北南港展覽館。
artificial intelligence (AI) (n.) — 人工智慧。讓電腦模仿人類思考、學習與判斷的技術。
hardware (n.) — 硬體。電腦看得到、摸得到的部分，如晶片、螢幕、鍵盤；相對的是軟體（software）。
feature (n.) — 功能、特色。產品提供的某項能力，如「語音翻譯功能」。
NPU (Neural Processing Unit) (n.) — 神經處理器。專門負責 AI 運算的晶片，是 AI 電腦的「新大腦」。
TOPS (n.) — 每秒兆次運算。衡量 AI 晶片算力的單位，數字越大代表 AI 能力越強。
offline (adj./adv.) — 離線、不連網。不需要網際網路也能運作的狀態。
server (n.) — 伺服器。放在遠端資料中心、替大量使用者處理資料的強大電腦。
on-device (adj.) — 裝置端的、在本機上的。AI 運算直接在自己的電腦上完成，資料不外傳。
privacy (n.) — 隱私。個人資料不被他人取得的權利，是 AI 時代最受關注的議題之一。
battery life (n.) — 電池續航力。充飽一次電可以使用多久。
charger (n.) — 充電器。為電子裝置補充電力的器材。
generative AI (n.) — 生成式 AI。會「產生」文字、圖片、聲音等內容的 AI，像會回答問題的店員。
agentic AI / AI agent (n.) — 代理式 AI／AI 代理人。能主動跨軟體替你完成任務的 AI，像私人秘書。
supply chain (n.) — 供應鏈。從原料、晶片到組裝成品的整條生產合作網絡，台灣是全球科技供應鏈的核心。
launch (n./v.) — 上市、發表。新產品正式推出販售。
