✅ 多選題日
Week 1 · 心理情緒類 · 12 分鐘
📝 多選題策略：先找「一定對」，再刪「一定錯」
📖 閱讀短文
Emotional regulation refers to the ability to manage one's feelings in response to different situations. People who struggle with emotional regulation may show signs of aggression or irritability. Research indicates that childhood trauma often makes individuals more prone to emotional outbursts in later life. Fortunately, techniques such as deep breathing, journaling, and speaking with a counselor can help people process their feelings. These methods allow emotions to be expressed in a healthy way, reducing the risk of harmful behavior over time.
🔍 判斷以下敘述，哪幾項符合文章內容？（多選）
  • (A) 情緒調節指的是在不同情境下管理自己感受的能力。
  • (B) 有攻擊性的人必定有過嚴重的童年創傷。
  • (C) 童年創傷可能讓人長大後更容易情緒失控。
  • (D) 寫日記是幫助人們處理情緒的方法之一。
  • (E) 文章認為情緒表達只有在諮商室中才是安全的。
查看答案 ▼
✅ 正確答案：ACD
(A) ✓ 文章第一句直接定義情緒調節。
(B) ✗ 文章說 trauma「often makes prone to」，是「容易」，不是「必定」。
(C) ✓ "childhood trauma often makes individuals more prone to emotional outbursts"
(D) ✓ journaling（寫日記）明確列出。
(E) ✗ 文章說「allow emotions to be expressed in a healthy way」，不限諮商室。

📝 句型改錯日
Week 1 · 心理情緒類 · 10 分鐘
🔧 找出錯誤並改正（各句只有一處錯誤）
1. She is very prone of making mistakes under pressure.
查看答案 ▼
of → to
prone to + V-ing，固定搭配。✏️ She is very prone to making mistakes under pressure.
2. The music evoked me some childhood memories.
查看答案 ▼
evoked me → evoked
evoke 不以人為受詞，直接接事物。✏️ The music evoked some childhood memories in me.
3. His aggression was trigger by the unfair treatment.
查看答案 ▼
trigger → triggered
被動語態需要過去分詞。✏️ His aggression was triggered by the unfair treatment.
4. The two students have contradicted opinions about the issue.
查看答案 ▼
contradicted → contradictory
修飾名詞要用形容詞。✏️ The two students have contradictory opinions about the issue.
🌟 本日句型重點
be prone to + N / V-ing（容易…）
He is prone to anxiety when facing difficult situations.
Teenagers are prone to being influenced by their peers.

📖 短文閱讀日
Week 1 · 心理情緒類 · 15 分鐘
📖 閱讀短文（約 160 字）
Music has long been recognized as a powerful tool for emotional healing. Research shows that listening to music can evoke deep emotions and even help reduce the residual effects of stress and trauma. When we hear a familiar song, certain memories or feelings are often brought to the surface. Scientists have found that music therapy is especially effective for individuals who are prone to anxiety or depression. It provides a safe space for emotions to be expressed without triggering aggression or outbursts. Unlike some medical treatments, music therapy has very few side effects and can be practiced daily at home. In schools across Taiwan, teachers have begun introducing short music sessions to help students manage irritability during exam periods. The goal is not to eliminate negative feelings, but to teach healthy emotional regulation—a skill that is valuable throughout one's entire life.
Q1. 多選：以下哪些敘述符合文章？
  • (A) 音樂治療對容易焦慮的人特別有效。
  • (B) 音樂治療可以完全消除負面情緒。
  • (C) 台灣的老師在考試期間引入音樂幫助學生。
  • (D) 聆聽音樂可以喚起情感與記憶。
  • (E) 音樂治療的副作用和藥物治療相同。
查看答案 ▼
✅ ACD
(A) ✓ "especially effective for individuals who are prone to anxiety"
(B) ✗ "goal is not to eliminate negative feelings"
(C) ✓ 台灣老師引入音樂課程直接提到。
(D) ✓ "evoke deep emotions…certain memories or feelings are brought to the surface"
(E) ✗ "very few side effects"，與藥物無關。
Q2. 單字題：文中 "evoke" 意思最接近？
  • (A) to create something completely new
  • (B) to bring out a feeling or memory
  • (C) to forget something painful
  • (D) to reduce stress through exercise
查看答案 ▼
✅ (B)
evoke = 喚起情感或記憶，答案為 B。

🔤 單字日
Week 4 · 社會經濟類 · 10 分鐘
monopoly/məˈnɒpəli/n.
壟斷；獨佔
The company holds a monopoly over the local water supply, giving it unlimited control over prices.
revenues/ˈrɛvənuːz/n.
收益；財政收入（常用複數）
The government's revenues from taxation increased significantly last year.
inclination/ˌɪnklɪˈneɪʃən/n.
傾向；意願；偏好
She has a strong inclination toward creative writing and literature rather than science.
contradictory/ˌkɒntrəˈdɪktəri/adj.
矛盾的；相反的
The two witnesses gave contradictory accounts of what happened at the scene.
indulge/ɪnˈdʌldʒ/v.
縱容；沉溺於（後接 in）
It is perfectly fine to indulge in your favorite dessert once in a while.
📌 小提示：inclination 常與 toward 搭配。indulge 後接 in + N/V-ing

In many modern economies, a small number of large corporations dominate entire sectors, creating what economists call monopolies. These arrangements can have contradictory effects: while they may drive innovation in some cases, they often suppress competition, raise prices, and limit consumer choice. Government revenues can also be affected, as powerful companies find ways to minimize their tax obligations. Regulators worldwide are increasingly showing an inclination to step in and enforce stricter rules to protect fair competition.

📖 短文閱讀日
Week 4 · 社會經濟類 · 15 分鐘
📖 閱讀短文（約 160 字）
The relationship between governments and large corporations is one of the most debated topics in modern economics. On one hand, successful businesses generate revenues that flow into the national treasury through taxation, funding public services like education and healthcare. On the other hand, when corporations gain a monopoly over essential goods, citizens may find themselves at the mercy of profit-driven decisions. History has depicted many cases where unchecked corporate power led to exploitation of ordinary people. In response, governments have legitimately used antitrust laws to break up monopolies and protect fair competition. Critics, however, argue that these contradictory approaches—encouraging business growth while simultaneously restraining it—create economic instability. The challenge is to balance freedom and fairness in a way that benefits all citizens, not just those who have the inclination and resources to indulge in wealth accumulation.
Q1. 多選：以下哪些符合文章？
  • (A) 企業透過納稅為國家財政做出貢獻。
  • (B) 當企業壟斷必需品時，消費者可能受損。
  • (C) 文章認為政府所有的干預都是無效的。
  • (D) 反壟斷法是政府合法使用的手段之一。
  • (E) 批評者認為鼓勵企業成長與同時限制企業的做法相互矛盾。
查看答案 ▼
✅ ABDE
(C) ✗ 文章說政府「legitimately used antitrust laws」，肯定了某些干預的正當性。
Q2. "at the mercy of" 最接近的意思是？
  • (A) grateful to（感謝）
  • (B) protected by（受到保護）
  • (C) completely dependent on, with no control（完全受制於）
  • (D) in competition with（與…競爭）
查看答案 ▼
✅ (C)
at the mercy of = 任由…擺布，毫無抵抗能力

🏆 四週綜合多選挑戰
Special · 跨主題整合 · 15 分鐘
📖 綜合閱讀短文（融合四週主題）
Human progress has always come with both benefits and contradictory consequences. The development of modern technology has facilitated communication and scientific discovery, but it has also disrupted traditional ways of life, sometimes triggering anxiety and irritability in those who struggle to adapt. Research with insights from psychology suggests that people prone to stress are more vulnerable to emotional trauma during rapid social change. At the same time, economic growth has expanded government revenues and improved living standards for many citizens. However, when a single company gains a monopoly over essential resources, it can confine consumer choice and outweigh public benefit with private profit. History has depicted many such cases. Conservation of both natural environments and human well-being, experts argue, should outweigh the pursuit of endless economic growth. Fostering a balance between progress and sustainability is not just desirable—it is an inevitability if future generations are to thrive.
🔍 綜合多選：哪幾項符合文章？（多選）
  • (A) 容易有壓力的人，在快速社會變遷中可能更容易受到情緒創傷。
  • (B) 科技的發展讓傳統生活方式更加穩定。
  • (C) 當一家公司壟斷重要資源，消費者的選擇可能受到限制。
  • (D) 文章主張，對自然環境和人類福祉的保育，其重要性應超過無止盡的經濟追求。
  • (E) 文章認為科技進步完全沒有負面影響。
查看答案 ▼
✅ ACD
(B) ✗ "disrupted traditional ways of life"，科技顛覆了傳統生活，並非更穩定。
(E) ✗ 文章說科技帶來好處但也有矛盾後果。
🔤 單字測驗：選出正確答案

    (A) insights　(B) irritability　(C) revenues　(D) conservation
查看答案 ▼
✅ (B) irritability
trigger irritability = 引發易怒情緒，語境最合適。

✍️ 寫作句型收尾日
Special · 30 天完結 · 15 分鐘
📚 30 天學過的 6 大句型整理
① be prone to + N/V-ing
Students are prone to feeling anxious before major exams.
② Not only…but also…
Technology has not only improved our lives but also created new challenges.
③ It is + adj + that + S + V
It is essential that we make conservation a national priority.
④ While + S + V, S + V（對比）
While monopolies can drive innovation, they often harm consumers.
⑤ A rather than B（而不是）
We should regulate our emotions rather than suppressing them completely.
⑥ The benefits outweigh the costs
The long-term benefits of investing in education far outweigh the short-term costs.
✍️ 最終挑戰：任選以下一個主題，用 3–5 句話寫一小段
主題選項：
A. 科技對人類生活的影響（正面與負面）
B. 為什麼保育環境比追求經濟成長更重要
C. 面對壓力時，情緒管理為什麼很重要

📝 要求：至少用到 2 個以上今天整理的句型，以及至少 3 個這 30 天學過的單字。
查看範例答案（主題 A）▼
Technology has not only facilitated global communication but also disrupted many traditional industries. While it has improved people's lives in many ways, it has also triggered anxiety and irritability in those who struggle to keep up with rapid change. It is essential that governments and citizens work together to ensure that the scope of technological development remains wholesome and beneficial to all. The long-term benefits of responsible innovation far outweigh the short-term costs of careful regulation.

🔑 使用句型：not only…but also / while / it is essential that / outweigh
使用單字：facilitated, disrupted, triggered, irritability, scope, wholesome
