Eco-Friendly High-Tech Fabrics in Today’s Swimsuits

現代泳裝中的環保高科技面料
林威老師與 AI agent 團隊製作

【區塊一】英文文章原文

英文文章原文 (Original Article)

Currently, the most advanced materials used in swimwear are innovative forms of polyester, such as Iterative® Polyester, and new composite functional fabrics that blend polyester with fibers like spandex or PBT (polybutylene terephthalate). These fabrics are designed to be lightweight and to repel water, so they reduce drag and help swimmers move more smoothly in the pool or ocean. Because they absorb very little water, they stay lighter than traditional materials during swimming and feel less heavy on the body.

These advanced fabrics also include special finishes and treatments that give them extra functions. Many modern polyester‑based swimwear fabrics are quick‑drying, so the swimsuit does not stay wet for a long time after you leave the water, which helps reduce bacteria growth and bad smells. They can also be made with antibacterial and mildew-resistant properties, and some blends are designed to feel cool to the touch or help regulate skin temperature in hot weather. At the same time, adding elastic fibers like spandex keeps the swimsuit stretchy, comfortable, and close‑fitting, even after many uses.

In addition, many of these high‑tech fabrics use recycled polyester or other regenerated fibers, which makes them more environmentally friendly than older materials. Brands now create swimwear from recycled plastic bottles or discarded fishing nets, helping reduce waste and pollution in the oceans. Because these new fabrics also resist chlorine, salt, and sunlight, they last longer without fading or losing their shape, so they are gradually replacing traditional nylon and neoprene in many high‑end swimsuits. As a result, advanced polyester and composite functional fabrics have become the mainstream choice for modern performance and fashion swimwear.

【區塊二】八句精讀

八句精讀 (8 Key Sentence Analyses)

1 核心主題句

Currently, the most advanced materials used in swimwear are innovative forms of polyester...
目前，泳裝中最先進的材料是各種創新形式的聚酯纖維……

文法解析：本句主詞為 the most advanced materials，其後接著過去分詞片語 used in swimwear 作為後置修飾語，修飾材料。動詞為 are。

advanced 先進的 swimwear 泳裝

2 目的與結果句

These fabrics are designed to be lightweight and to repel water, so they reduce drag and help swimmers move more smoothly...
這些面料被設計得非常輕盈且疏水，因此能減少阻力並幫助游泳者更流暢地移動……

文法解析：are designed to be 為被動語態，表示「被設計來……」。so 引導結果對等子句。help + O + V 為使役動詞用法，move 前省略了 to。

repel water 疏水/撥水 reduce drag 減少阻力

3 原因對比句

Because they absorb very little water, they stay lighter than traditional materials during swimming...
因為它們吸收的水分極少，在游泳過程中它們比傳統材料更輕……

文法解析：Because 引導原因副詞子句。stay lighter than 使用連綴動詞 stay 搭配比較級形容詞，描述狀態的持續。

absorb 吸收 traditional 傳統的

4 結果補語句

...the swimsuit does not stay wet for a long time after you leave the water, which helps reduce bacteria growth...
……泳衣在你離開水後不會長時間保持潮濕，這有助於減少細菌滋生……

文法解析：which 引導非限定關係子句，修飾前面「泳衣不會長時間潮濕」這整件事，動詞 helps 須用單數。

quick-drying 快速乾燥 bacteria growth 細菌滋生

5 並列結構句

They can also be made with antibacterial and mildew-resistant properties, and some blends are designed to feel cool to the touch...
它們還可以具備抗菌和防霉特性，且某些混紡布料被設計成觸感涼爽……

文法解析：本句使用對等連接詞 and 連結兩個被動語態的子句。be made with 表示「具備/由……製成」。

antibacterial 抗菌的 mildew-resistant 防霉的

6 動名詞主詞句

At the same time, adding elastic fibers like spandex keeps the swimsuit stretchy, comfortable, and close-fitting...
同時，加入像氨綸這樣的彈性纖維能讓泳衣保持彈性、舒適且貼身……

文法解析：adding elastic fibers... 為動名詞片語當主詞，動詞須用單數 keeps。keep + O + adj. 為常見句型。

elastic fibers 彈性纖維 close-fitting 貼身的

7 關係代名詞非限定用法

...many of these high-tech fabrics use recycled polyester or other regenerated fibers, which makes them more environmentally friendly...
……許多這類高科技面料使用回收聚酯或其他再生纖維，這使得它們比舊材料更環保。

文法解析：which 引導的子句修飾前面的主要子句（使用再生纖維這件事），這是一種非限定用法的擴展，用來補充說明結果。

regenerated fibers 再生纖維 environmentally friendly 環保的

8 無法避免的結果句

Because these new fabrics also resist chlorine, salt, and sunlight, they last longer without fading or losing their shape...
因為這些新面料還能抵抗氯、鹽和陽光，它們的使用壽命更長，且不會褪色或變形……

文法解析：Because 引導原因子句。last longer 表示持續更久。without + V-ing 表示「沒有……的情況下」。

resist chlorine 抗氯 fade 褪色

【區塊四】文法解析

文法解析 (Grammar Analysis)

1. 被動語態 (Passive Voice)：be + p.p.

These fabrics are designed to be lightweight and to repel water...

句型：S + be + p.p. (+ by + O) (斜體粗體)

核心說明：被動語態用於強調「動作的承受者」，當執行者不明確、不重要或為了語氣連貫時使用。

使用規則：

  • 現在式被動：am/is/are + p.p.
  • 助動詞被動：can/must/should + be + p.p.
  • 表示「目的」時常接 to V：be designed to + V。

主題例句：

  • The swimsuit is made of recycled plastic bottles. (這件泳衣是由回收塑膠瓶製成的。)
  • These fabrics can be used for professional racing. (這些面料可用於專業競賽。)

學習提醒：不可及物動詞（如 happen, appear）沒有被動語態。

⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題：

✗ The new fabric is design to repel water.

✓ The new fabric is designed to repel water.

說明：被動語態必須使用過去分詞 (p.p.)。

1. The ocean ________ (pollute) by discarded fishing nets every year.
答案：is polluted

2. 關係代名詞非限定用法 (Non-restrictive Which)

...which helps reduce bacteria growth and bad smells.

句型：..., which + V ... (斜體粗體)

核心說明：逗號後的 which 可以用來修飾前面的「整件事」或「整個子句」，而不僅僅是一個單字。

使用規則：

  • 先行詞為整個子句時，動詞須用單數。
  • 這種用法具有補充說明的效果，常譯為「這件事使得……」。

主題例句：

  • The fabric dries quickly, which is important for swimmers. (布料乾得很快，這對游泳者來說很重要。)
  • Regenerated fibers are used, which reduces waste. (使用再生纖維，這減少了浪費。)

學習提醒：在這種非限定用法中，不可使用 that 替代 which。

⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題：

✗ He always late for practice, that annoys the coach.

✓ He always late for practice, which annoys the coach.

說明：修飾前句整件事的關係代名詞必須用 which，前面要加逗號。

1. The new swimsuit resists salt and sun, ________ makes it last longer.
答案：which

3. 動名詞片語當主詞 (Gerund as Subject)

At the same time, adding elastic fibers like spandex keeps the swimsuit stretchy...

句型：V-ing (+ O) + 單數V ... (斜體粗體)

核心說明：當我們想把一個「動作」當作整句話的主詞時，會將動詞改為 V-ing 形式（動名詞）。

使用規則：

  • 動名詞片語當主詞，視為「單數」。
  • 常用於描述一種習慣、事實或通則。

主題例句：

  • Choosing eco-friendly fabrics helps protect the ocean. (選擇環保面料有助於保護海洋。)
  • Swimming in the ocean requires high-performance gear. (在海中游泳需要高性能裝備。)

學習提醒：注意動詞時態，若是事實則常用現在式並加 -s。

⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題：

✗ Using recycled materials help the environment.

✓ Using recycled materials helps the environment.

說明：動名詞主詞視為單數，動詞需加 s。

1. ________ (Wear) high-tech swimsuits reduces water resistance.
答案：Wearing

名詞字尾

-tion/-sion/-ation 動作、過程或結果 — education（教育）、decision（決定）

-ness 狀態或性質 — happiness（快樂）、darkness（黑暗）

-ity/-ty 抽象概念或狀態 — ability（能力）、cruelty（殘酷）

-er/-or 執行某動作的人或物 — teacher（老師）、actor（演員）

-ist 信仰或專業的人 — artist（藝術家）、scientist（科學家）

-less 缺乏…的 — hopeless（絕望的）、powerless（無力的）

動詞字尾

-ize/-ise 使成為… — realize（實現）、industrialize（工業化）

副詞字尾

練文筆食材 (Writing Ingredients)

① be designed to + V（被設計用來……）
→ 適用：說明產品功能或政策目的時
例：The new app is designed to help students manage their time.

② not only ... but also ...（不僅……而且……）
→ 適用：強調多重優點時
例：The project not only reduces costs but also improves quality.

③ ..., which + V ...（非限定修飾，修飾前句整件事）
→ 適用：補充說明動作帶來的結果
例：He finished the task ahead of schedule, which impressed his boss.

④ Because of + N, S + V ...（由於……，……）
→ 適用：開門見山點出原因
例：Because of the high price, few people can afford this luxury car.

⑤ In addition, ...（此外……）
→ 適用：增加論點或資訊時
例：In addition, the system provides real-time data analysis.

⑥ At the same time, ...（同時……）
→ 適用：平衡不同觀點或補充並行事實時
例：At the same time, we must consider the potential risks.

⑦ As a result, ...（因此……）
→ 適用：引導出最後的結論或結果
例：As a result, the company decided to expand its business abroad.

⑧ adding ... keeps ...（動名詞主詞 + 保持……）
→ 適用：說明採取某動作能維持某種狀態
例：Adding more features keeps the software competitive.

⑨ help + O + (to) V（幫助某人做某事）
→ 適用：描述解決方案的效果
例：The exercise plan helps her maintain a healthy weight.

⑩ stay + adj. than ...（比……更保持……狀態）
→ 適用：進行性能對比時
例：The new battery stays charged longer than the old one.
