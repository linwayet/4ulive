【區塊一】英文文章原文

英文文章原文 Original Article

Newswatch, Study Finds Video Games May Benefit the Brain.

For decades, parents have warned their children that video games would rot their brains. However, recent scientific studies suggest that moderate gaming may actually enhance cognitive abilities rather than harm them. Research conducted by the National Institutes of Health examined nearly 2,000 children and found that those who played video games regularly outperformed non-gamers on tests measuring attention, memory, and impulse control. Brain imaging further revealed increased activity in regions responsible for decision-making and information processing. Similarly, a University of Colorado Boulder study reported that adults who gamed for a few hours weekly demonstrated improved spatial reasoning and faster reaction times. Participants who played over five hours per week performed cognitively as though they were years younger. Nevertheless, researchers cautioned that these benefits apply only to moderate gaming. Excessive playtime can still lead to negative consequences, including sleep problems and reduced physical activity. In conclusion, while video games have long been viewed as harmful distractions, science now indicates that playing them wisely might provide our brains with a beneficial workout.

Artificial womb technology offers hope but sparks debate.

Scientists are developing artificial womb technology that could revolutionize care for extremely premature babies. Known as Aqua Womb, this device is essentially a sealed bag filled with heated artificial amniotic fluid. Inside, the infant is connected to an artificial placenta that supplies oxygen and nutrients, mimicking the conditions within the mother's body. According to researchers at Radboud University in the Netherlands, babies born between 24 and 28 weeks often face life-threatening complications. By extending fetal development for just a few more weeks, their survival rates could improve dramatically. However, this revolutionary technology raises profound ethical concerns. Critics worry that it could blur the boundaries of when life begins and change traditional concepts of motherhood. Additionally, the high cost may limit access to wealthier families, potentially widening social inequality. Some also fear that commercialization could lead to misuse. While artificial wombs hold enormous promise, society must carefully weigh their benefits against these ethical challenges.

【區塊二】8句精讀

八句精讀 8 Key Sentence Analyses

①句子一

However, recent scientific studies suggest that moderate gaming may actually enhance cognitive abilities rather than harm them.

然而，近來的科學研究顯示，適度玩電玩實際上可能提升認知能力，而不是傷害它們。

文法解析：這句是含有 that 名詞子句的複合句。主要動詞是 suggest，後面的 that 子句說明研究「顯示」的內容。rather than 連接 enhance 和 harm 兩個動詞，形成「不是……而是……」的對比。may 表示可能性，語氣比 will 保守，符合科學報導的寫法。

moderate：適度的cognitive abilities：認知能力rather than：而不是

②句子二

Research conducted by the National Institutes of Health examined nearly 2,000 children and found that those who played video games regularly outperformed non-gamers on tests measuring attention, memory, and impulse control.

由美國國家衛生研究院進行的研究檢視了將近兩千名兒童，並發現經常玩電玩的孩子在測量注意力、記憶力和衝動控制的測驗中表現優於非玩家。

文法解析：Research conducted by... 使用過去分詞 conducted 簡化形容詞子句，等於 research that was conducted by...。主要動詞有 examined 和 found，兩者由 and 連接。that those who played... outperformed... 是 found 的受詞子句，其中 who played video games regularly 是形容詞子句，修飾 those。measuring attention... 是現在分詞片語，修飾 tests。

conducted：進行的outperformed：表現優於impulse control：衝動控制

③句子三

Brain imaging further revealed increased activity in regions responsible for decision-making and information processing.

大腦影像進一步顯示，負責決策與資訊處理的區域活動增加。

文法解析：這句主詞是 Brain imaging，動詞是 revealed，受詞是 increased activity。responsible for... 是形容詞片語，放在 regions 後面修飾它。decision-making 和 information processing 都是動名詞形成的名詞片語，作為 for 的受詞。

revealed：顯示regions：區域information processing：資訊處理

④句子四

Participants who played over five hours per week performed cognitively as though they were years younger.

每週玩超過五小時的參與者，在認知表現上好像年輕了好幾歲。

文法解析：who played over five hours per week 是形容詞子句，修飾 Participants。as though 引導副詞子句，表示「彷彿、好像」。they were years younger 使用過去式，表達與現實不完全相同的假設語氣。cognitively 是副詞，修飾 performed。

participants：參與者as though：彷彿cognitively：在認知上

⑤句子五

Excessive playtime can still lead to negative consequences, including sleep problems and reduced physical activity.

過度的遊玩時間仍可能導致負面後果，包括睡眠問題和身體活動減少。

文法解析：can 表示可能性，still 強調即使前文提到好處，風險仍存在。lead to 是動詞片語，to 是介係詞，所以後面接名詞 negative consequences。including... 是介系詞片語，用來補充說明 consequences 的例子。reduced 是過去分詞作形容詞，表示「被減少的」。

excessive：過度的lead to：導致consequences：後果

⑥句子六

Scientists are developing artificial womb technology that could revolutionize care for extremely premature babies.

科學家正在開發人工子宮科技，這項科技可能徹底改變極早產兒的照護方式。

文法解析：are developing 是現在進行式，表示目前正在進行的研發。that could revolutionize... 是形容詞子句，修飾 artificial womb technology。could 表示可能性，也保留科技尚未完全成熟的語氣。care for... 中的 for 是介係詞，表示「對……的照護」。

artificial：人工的revolutionize：徹底改變premature babies：早產兒

⑦句子七

Inside, the infant is connected to an artificial placenta that supplies oxygen and nutrients, mimicking the conditions within the mother's body.

在裡面，嬰兒會連接到一個供應氧氣和營養的人造胎盤，模擬母體內的環境。

文法解析：is connected to 是被動語態，表示嬰兒「被連接到」人造胎盤。that supplies oxygen and nutrients 是形容詞子句，修飾 placenta。mimicking... 是現在分詞構句，補充說明前面整個裝置的功能。within the mother's body 是介系詞片語，表示位置。

infant：嬰兒placenta：胎盤mimicking：模擬

⑧句子八

While artificial wombs hold enormous promise, society must carefully weigh their benefits against these ethical challenges.

雖然人工子宮前景非常可觀，社會仍必須謹慎權衡它們的好處與這些倫理挑戰。

文法解析：While 在此引導讓步副詞子句，意思接近 although。主要子句是 society must carefully weigh...，must 表示強烈必要性。weigh A against B 是重要片語，表示「權衡 A 與 B」。carefully 是副詞，修飾 weigh，凸顯公共議題需要審慎判斷。

hold enormous promise：前景可觀weigh...against...：權衡ethical challenges：倫理挑戰

【分詞簡化關係子句】Reduced Relative Clauses 分詞簡化關係子句

【引句 Quote】

Research conducted by the National Institutes of Health examined nearly 2,000 children.

【句型】

N + V-ed / V-ing + ... + V

【核心說明】

分詞片語可以放在名詞後面，功能類似形容詞子句，使句子更精簡。若名詞與動作是被動關係，使用過去分詞；若是主動或正在進行的關係，使用現在分詞。

【使用規則】

  • 過去分詞表示「被……的」，如 research conducted by...。
  • 現在分詞表示「正在……的」或「主動……的」，如 tests measuring attention。
  • 化簡時通常刪除關係代名詞與 be 動詞。
  • 分詞片語必須緊靠它修飾的名詞，避免修飾對象不清楚。

【主題例句】

Children tested in the study showed different levels of attention.
（研究中接受測試的兒童展現不同程度的注意力。）

【學習提醒】

看到名詞後面直接接 V-ing 或 V-ed，不要急著把它當主要動詞；先判斷它是不是修飾前面名詞的分詞片語。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ Research was conducted by the institute examined 2,000 children.

✓ Research conducted by the institute examined 2,000 children.（conducted 是分詞，不可再保留 was。）

試題：The device ________ by the team may help premature babies.
A. developing B. developed C. develops D. develop

答案：B。說明：device 與 develop 是被動關係，應用過去分詞 developed。

【形容詞子句】Relative Clauses 關係子句

【引句 Quote】

Scientists are developing artificial womb technology that could revolutionize care for extremely premature babies.

【句型】

N + who / which / that + V + ...

【核心說明】

形容詞子句用來補充或限定前面的名詞，也就是先行詞。本文中的 that could revolutionize... 修飾 artificial womb technology，說明這種科技可能具有的影響。

【使用規則】

  • who 通常修飾人，which 修飾事物或動物。
  • that 可修飾人或事物，但多用在限定子句。
  • 逗號隔開的非限定子句通常不用 that。
  • 關係子句中的動詞必須和其真正主詞一致。

【主題例句】

The study that examined young gamers attracted public attention.
（那項檢視年輕玩家的研究引起大眾注意。）

【學習提醒】

關係子句不是獨立句子，它必須依附在名詞後面。寫作時要先確認先行詞，再選 who、which 或 that。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ The technology could help babies that was born too early.

✓ The technology could help babies who were born too early.（babies 是人且為複數，應用 who were。）

試題：Brain regions ________ process information may become more active.
A. who B. which C. where D. what

答案：B。說明：regions 是事物，且關係代名詞在子句中作主詞，應選 which。

【讓步副詞子句】Concessive Clauses 讓步子句

【引句 Quote】

While artificial wombs hold enormous promise, society must carefully weigh their benefits against these ethical challenges.

【句型】

Although / While + S + V, S + V

【核心說明】

讓步副詞子句表示「雖然……但是……」，用來承認一個事實，同時提出主要觀點。本文用 While 承認人工子宮有巨大潛力，再指出社會仍需面對倫理挑戰。

【使用規則】

  • although、though、while 都可表示讓步，正式寫作常用 although 或 while。
  • 英文中 although 和 but 通常不要同時放在同一句的兩個子句前。
  • 讓步子句放句首時，後面通常加逗號。
  • while 也可表示「然而」，需依上下文判斷。

【主題例句】

Although video games may improve attention, excessive playtime can harm sleep.
（雖然電玩可能改善注意力，過度遊玩仍可能傷害睡眠。）

【學習提醒】

讓步句的重點通常在主要子句。寫作時可用它來呈現平衡觀點，特別適合討論科技、健康與社會議題。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ Although artificial wombs are promising, but they raise ethical concerns.

✓ Although artificial wombs are promising, they raise ethical concerns.（although 已有「雖然」語意，不需再加 but。）

試題：______ the device may save lives, it also raises difficult questions.
A. Because B. While C. So D. Unless

答案：B。說明：前後語意形成讓步與轉折，應使用 While。

折疊四：練文筆食材⌄

① rather than + V（而不是……）
→ 適用：對比兩種可能結果。
例：The policy should protect students rather than limit their choices.

② those who + V（那些……的人）
→ 適用：指稱具有共同特徵的一群人。
例：Those who sleep well often perform better in class.

③ be responsible for + N/V-ing（負責……；是……的原因）
→ 適用：說明功能或責任。
例：This part of the brain is responsible for memory.

④ as though + S + V（彷彿……）
→ 適用：描述看似如此但不一定真實的情況。
例：He spoke as though he had already solved the problem.

⑤ lead to + N/V-ing（導致……）
→ 適用：說明原因與結果。
例：Excessive stress can lead to poor sleep.

⑥ while + S + V, S + V（雖然……，……）
→ 適用：呈現平衡觀點。
例：While technology is useful, it also raises new problems.

⑦ be connected to + N（連接到……；與……有關）
→ 適用：描述裝置連結或概念關聯。
例：Good study habits are connected to long-term success.

⑧ according to + N（根據……）
→ 適用：引用資料來源或研究說法。
例：According to the survey, many teenagers use phones before bed.

⑨ by + V-ing（藉由……）
→ 適用：說明方法或手段。
例：By reviewing notes daily, students can remember more.

⑩ potentially + V-ing（可能會……）
→ 適用：說明尚未確定但可能發生的影響。
例：The new rule could help students, potentially reducing exam stress.

⑪ provide A with B（提供 A 某物）
→ 適用：說明供應或支援。
例：Libraries provide students with quiet places to study.

⑫ weigh A against B（權衡 A 與 B）
→ 適用：討論利弊、風險與價值。
例：We must weigh convenience against privacy.
