🔴 考卷原題 社會心理
inferior
/ɪnˈfɪəriər/
adj.
低劣的；自卑的 （← 考卷 Q14 正解）
🌱 字根 infer-（低下）+ -ior（比較級後綴）
衍生詞
inferiority n. 低劣；自卑感
inferiority complex n. 自卑情結
superior adj. 優秀的；上司
superiority n. 優越感
例句
Scrolling through social media can make people feel inferior to others.
滑社群媒體可能讓人覺得自己不如別人。
She developed a deep sense of inferiority after years of being compared to her sister.
多年來被拿來和姊姊比較，她深深產生了自卑感。
No product is inferior simply because it costs less—quality varies independently of price.
沒有哪個產品單純因為便宜就是劣質品——品質與價格無必然關係。

2文法重點 · 3 Points
⚡ 根據你的考卷失誤，挑選最容易短期進步的題型
Point 1：固定介係詞片語
你在 Q29 錯選了 on，正解是 to → pose a threat to
高頻動詞片語中的介係詞是固定的，不能替換。以下三個必背：

pose a threat to sb/sth 對⋯構成威脅
have problems with + V-ing 在⋯有問題
on account of + N 因為⋯（= because of）
Air pollution poses a serious threat to public health in major cities.
空氣污染對大城市的公共衛生構成嚴重威脅。
Many students have problems with managing their time effectively during exam season.
許多學生在考試季節難以有效管理時間。
The match was postponed on account of heavy rain.
比賽因大雨而延期。
Point 2：Only + 介詞片語 → 倒裝句
你在 Q31 錯選了 to help，正解是 could she help（助動詞提前）
當 only 搭配介詞片語（in this way / after / then / by + V-ing）置於句首時，主句必須「助動詞/be動詞 + 主詞」倒裝：

Only + 介詞片語，助動詞 + 主詞 + 動詞
Only in this way could she help her poor parents share the burden.
只有用這種方式，她才能幫助貧困的父母分擔重擔。
Only after the experiment did scientists realize the true danger.
直到實驗之後，科學家才意識到真正的危險。
Only by reading widely can you improve your English significantly.
只有廣泛閱讀，你才能大幅提升英文能力。
Point 3：urge / insist / suggest 後接形式
你在 Q38 答對（go），但 Q35 的 urge 系列仍需鞏固
以下動詞後接 that 子句時，子句動詞用原形（假設語氣，should 可省略）：

insist / urge / suggest / demand that S (should) V-原形

但 urge sb. to V（直接受詞後用不定詞）是另一種常見句型。
His parents insisted that he go back to school to learn social skills.
他的父母堅持讓他回學校學習社交技能。（go 原形，非 went）
The doctor suggested that she reduce her sugar intake immediately.
醫生建議她立刻減少糖分攝取。（reduce 原形）
Peace urged Rejoice to return to school and pursue her dream.
Peace 力勸 Rejoice 回學校追求她的夢想。（urge sb. to V）
3閱讀練習
📏 長度約為 115 年學測閱讀題的 1/2，主題與今日單字高度相關
The Hidden Cost of Clickbait Culture
約 160 字 · 中等難度 · 主題：媒體素養 & 心理健康

In today's digital world, clickbait headlines have become alarmingly prevalent. These headlines are designed to trigger strong emotions—curiosity, fear, or anger—and lure users into clicking links that reveal little of value. The real purpose is financial: every click generates revenue for the website.

Researchers have found that constant exposure to clickbait poses a serious threat to critical thinking. Users who consume misleading content frequently become skeptical of even high-quality journalism. This erosion of trust has a binding effect on society—it ties people to inferior information sources while genuine knowledge becomes harder to access.

The problem is especially serious for young readers. Studies show that teenagers who rely on social media for news are more likely to develop a sense of information poverty—surrounded by content yet lacking real understanding. As misinformation continues to mushroom online, developing strong media literacy skills has never been more essential.

Q1. According to the passage, why do websites use clickbait headlines?
(A) To educate readers about important social issues
(B) To generate income through user clicks
(C) To promote high-quality journalism
(D) To help users develop critical thinking
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
✅ 正解：(B)
文章明確指出「every click generates revenue for the website」（每次點擊都為網站產生收益），選 (B) 最直接。(A)(C)(D) 均與文意相反。
Q2. What does the word "prevalent" (paragraph 1) most closely mean?
(A) rare and unusual
(B) widely common and widespread
(C) extremely dangerous
(D) gradually disappearing
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
✅ 正解：(B)
prevalent = 普遍的、盛行的，即 widely common。文中描述 clickbait 已是「令人警惕地普遍」，對應 (B)。
Q3. What effect does clickbait have on young readers, according to the passage?
(A) It improves their ability to find reliable sources
(B) It encourages them to read more quality news
(C) It leads to a lack of genuine understanding despite access to content
(D) It helps them become more skeptical in a positive way
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
✅ 正解：(C)
文章提到年輕讀者易陷入「information poverty」——被內容包圍卻缺乏真正的理解。這正是 (C) 的描述。注意 (D) 是干擾項，文中的 skeptical 是負面的（不信任好的新聞）。
4單字複習測驗 · 5題
1. Comparing yourself to others on social media can make you feel _____ and damage your self-esteem.
(A) superior
(B) inferior
(C) rewarding
(D) flexible
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
✅ 正解：(B) inferior
inferior（自卑的、低劣的）是 Q14 的考卷原題。damage self-esteem（損害自尊）配合「感到低人一等」，選 (B)。(A) superior 是相反詞。
2. The new study _____ a surprising link between sleep deprivation and poor academic performance.
(A) conceals
(B) acquires
(C) reveals
(D) baits
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
✅ 正解：(C) reveals
reveal（揭露）對應考卷 Q18 的句型。(A) conceals 是反義詞（隱藏），(B) acquires（獲得），(D) baits（誘騙）均不符語意。
3. The spread of misinformation online _____ a major threat _____ democratic values.
(A) causes; for
(B) poses; to
(C) makes; on
(D) creates; of
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
✅ 正解：(B) poses; to
固定片語 pose a threat to（對⋯構成威脅）。這正是你在 Q29 答錯的考點！牢記 pose + to 這個組合。
4. _____ by studying together with classmates _____ she finally overcome her fear of math.
(A) Only; did
(B) Just; could
(C) Only; she could
(D) Only; that
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
✅ 正解：(A) Only; did
Only + 介詞片語（by studying together）置首 → 主句倒裝：did she finally overcome。這正是 Q31 的倒裝句考點！注意 (C) 錯誤，倒裝句不保留正常語序。
5. Small bubble tea shops have _____ all over Taiwan in the past decade.
(A) shoveled
(B) tugged
(C) fueled
(D) mushroomed
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
✅ 正解：(D) mushroomed
mushroom（動詞）= 如雨後春筍般迅速增加。這是 Q16 的考卷原題，描述咖啡店遍布全市，同樣的概念套用在珍奶店上。(A) shoveled（鏟）(B) tugged（拉）(C) fueled（燃燒驅動）均不符。
