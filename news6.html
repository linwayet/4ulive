Why Are Expansion Joints Necessary in Bridges?

Expansion joints are crucial components in bridge engineering because bridges constantly respond to environmental forces that cause their materials to move. Bridge materials such as steel and concrete expand when heated and contract when cooled, and without expansion joints, these thermal movements would create massive internal stresses leading to cracking, spalling, or even structural failure. A typical bridge can expand or contract by several inches during seasonal temperature fluctuations, making these joints essential for absorbing stress without compromising structural integrity. Additionally, expansion joints accommodate movements induced by traffic loading, allowing dynamic deflections when heavy vehicles cross the bridge without introducing stress concentrations at crucial connection points.

The principle of thermal expansion and contraction is applied in the engineering of large building structures to prevent damage caused by temperature differences. When bridge components absorb heat, their particles move faster and spread apart, causing the structure to expand; conversely, when temperatures drop, the materials contract. This linear relationship is primarily determined by the thermal expansion coefficient of the bridge materials, meaning the strain value is proportional to the extent of temperature change. Different materials used in bridges—concrete and steel—have different thermal expansion coefficients, leading to varying degrees of expansion during the heating process. Engineers strategically design expansion joints to span gaps between structural elements, effectively absorbing these movements while accommodating shrinkage, creep effects, and temperature variations on reinforced and prestressed concrete, composite, and steel structures.

Modern expansion joints come in various types that can accommodate movements ranging from 30 to 1,000 millimeters, depending on the bridge's design requirements. These joints not only prevent structural damage but also protect bridges from moisture intrusion that could lead to corrosion and deterioration of concrete, thereby enhancing the bridge's longevity. Without proper expansion joints, bridges would experience cracking and deformation from repeated expansion and contraction cycles, load instability from heavy vehicles, and accelerated water damage that compromises safety. The expansion joint serves as the element under the most strain in any engineering structure, making its correct selection and installation vital for maintaining bridge safety and functionality under varying conditions.

8 Key Sentence Analyses

①Expansion joints are crucial components in bridge engineering because bridges constantly respond to environmental forces that cause their materials to move.

伸縮縫是橋梁工程中至關重要的組成部分，因為橋梁會不斷回應造成材料移動的環境力量。

文法解析：原因副詞子句 + 關係子句。主要子句說明伸縮縫的重要性，because 引導原因，解釋為何它們不可或缺。that cause their materials to move 修飾 environmental forces，讓讀者知道這些力量的具體作用。cause + O + to V 表示「使某人或某物做某事」，其中 to 是不定詞，不是介係詞。

亮點詞彙：crucial：至關重要的components：組成部分environmental forces：環境力量

②Bridge materials such as steel and concrete expand when heated and contract when cooled, and without expansion joints, these thermal movements would create massive internal stresses leading to cracking, spalling, or even structural failure.

鋼材與混凝土等橋梁材料受熱時會膨脹、冷卻時會收縮；若沒有伸縮縫，這些熱位移會產生巨大的內部應力，導致裂縫、剝落，甚至結構失效。

文法解析：省略型副詞子句 + 對等連接 + 分詞片語。when heated 和 when cooled 分別省略了 they are，讓句子更精簡。and 連接兩個完整概念，前半描述物理現象，後半說明後果。leading to... 是現在分詞片語，補充 massive internal stresses 會造成的結果。

亮點詞彙：thermal movements：熱位移internal stresses：內部應力structural failure：結構失效

③A typical bridge can expand or contract by several inches during seasonal temperature fluctuations, making these joints essential for absorbing stress without compromising structural integrity.

一般橋梁在季節性溫度波動期間可能膨脹或收縮數英吋，因此這些接縫對吸收應力而不損害結構完整性來說十分必要。

文法解析：主要子句 + 結果分詞構句。can expand or contract 表示可能發生的能力或情況。making these joints essential... 是現在分詞片語，用來表示前面整件事造成的結果。without + V-ing 表示「沒有做某事」，此處說明吸收應力時不會犧牲結構完整性。

亮點詞彙：fluctuations：波動absorbing stress：吸收應力structural integrity：結構完整性

④Additionally, expansion joints accommodate movements induced by traffic loading, allowing dynamic deflections when heavy vehicles cross the bridge without introducing stress concentrations at crucial connection points.

此外，伸縮縫能容納交通載重引發的位移，使重型車輛通過橋梁時能產生動態撓曲，而不會在關鍵連接點引入應力集中。

文法解析：分詞片語連續補充說明。induced by traffic loading 是過去分詞片語，修飾 movements，意思是「由交通載重引起的」。allowing dynamic deflections 表示伸縮縫帶來的效果。when heavy vehicles cross the bridge 是時間副詞子句，說明動態撓曲發生的時機。

亮點詞彙：accommodate：容納；適應dynamic deflections：動態撓曲stress concentrations：應力集中

⑤The principle of thermal expansion and contraction is applied in the engineering of large building structures to prevent damage caused by temperature differences.

熱膨脹與收縮的原理被應用在大型建築結構工程中，以防止溫差造成的損害。

文法解析：被動語態 + 不定詞表目的。is applied 表示「被應用」，強調原理本身，而非誰去應用它。to prevent damage 是目的不定詞，說明應用此原理的目的。caused by temperature differences 是過去分詞片語，修飾 damage，表示損害的來源。

亮點詞彙：principle：原理is applied：被應用temperature differences：溫差

⑥This linear relationship is primarily determined by the thermal expansion coefficient of the bridge materials, meaning the strain value is proportional to the extent of temperature change.

這種線性關係主要由橋梁材料的熱膨脹係數決定，這表示應變值與溫度變化的程度成比例。

文法解析：被動語態 + 補充說明分詞片語。is determined by 說明決定因素，by 後面接造成結果的來源。meaning... 是現在分詞片語，用來補充解釋前一句的工程含義。be proportional to 是固定搭配，to 是介係詞，後面接名詞片語 the extent of temperature change。

亮點詞彙：coefficient：係數strain value：應變值proportional to：與……成比例

⑦These joints not only prevent structural damage but also protect bridges from moisture intrusion that could lead to corrosion and deterioration of concrete, thereby enhancing the bridge's longevity.

這些接縫不僅能防止結構損害，也能保護橋梁免於受潮氣侵入影響；潮氣侵入可能導致鋼筋腐蝕與混凝土劣化，進而延長橋梁壽命。

文法解析：not only... but also... 對等結構 + 關係子句 + thereby 分詞片語。not only 與 but also 連接兩個動詞片語 prevent 和 protect，強調雙重功能。that could lead to... 修飾 moisture intrusion，說明水分侵入可能造成的後果。thereby enhancing... 表示「因此提升」，用正式語氣呈現結果。

亮點詞彙：moisture intrusion：水分侵入corrosion：腐蝕longevity：壽命；耐久性

⑧The expansion joint serves as the element under the most strain in any engineering structure, making its correct selection and installation vital for maintaining bridge safety and functionality under varying conditions.

伸縮縫是任何工程結構中承受最大應變的構件，因此正確選擇與安裝伸縮縫，對於在不同條件下維持橋梁安全與功能至關重要。

文法解析：主要子句 + 結果分詞構句。serves as 表示「擔任；起……作用」，後面接名詞補語。under the most strain 修飾 element，指出伸縮縫承受的壓力狀態。making its correct selection and installation vital... 把前面事實轉成結論，說明選型與安裝的重要性。

亮點詞彙：serves as：作為installation：安裝functionality：功能性

區塊四　文法解析

Grammar Focus

【分詞構句】Participial Construction 分詞構句

【引句 Quote】

A typical bridge can expand or contract by several inches during seasonal temperature fluctuations, making these joints essential for absorbing stress without compromising structural integrity.

【句型】

S + V ..., V-ing + O/C.

【核心說明】

分詞構句常用來補充說明主句的結果、原因、時間或伴隨狀態。本文中的 making these joints essential... 表示前面「橋梁會膨脹或收縮」這件事造成的結果。

這種寫法比直接使用 and this makes... 更簡潔，也更符合科普文章的書面語風格。

【使用規則】

  • 現在分詞 V-ing 常表示主動、同時發生或自然結果。
  • 若分詞片語表示結果，通常放在主要子句之後，前面加逗號。
  • 分詞片語的邏輯主詞要能從主句清楚判斷。
  • without + V-ing 表示「沒有做某事」，常用來補充限制條件。

【主題例句】

The bridge expanded in the heat, creating extra stress on its joints.
（橋梁在高溫中膨脹，因而在接縫上產生額外應力。）

【學習提醒】

不要把結果分詞構句誤解成主要動詞；它是補充資訊，主句仍然需要自己的主詞與動詞。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ The bridge expands during summer, makes the joints important.

✓ The bridge expands during summer, making the joints important.

說明：逗號後若沒有連接詞，不能直接放 makes；應改成現在分詞 making。

題目：The metal parts became hotter, ______ them to expand. A. cause B. caused C. causing D. to causing

答案：C。說明：此處要用現在分詞 causing 表示前面情況造成的結果。

【形容詞子句】Relative Clause 關係子句

【引句 Quote】

These joints not only prevent structural damage but also protect bridges from moisture intrusion that could lead to corrosion and deterioration of concrete, thereby enhancing the bridge's longevity.

【句型】

N + that/which + V ...

【核心說明】

形容詞子句用來修飾前面的名詞，使資訊更精確。句中的 that could lead to corrosion and deterioration of concrete 修飾 moisture intrusion，說明這種水分侵入可能造成什麼後果。

that 在此是關係代名詞，代表 moisture intrusion，並在子句中當主詞。

【使用規則】

  • 先行詞是事物時，可用 that 或 which 引導限定用法的形容詞子句。
  • 限定用法提供必要資訊，通常不加逗號。
  • 若關係代名詞在子句中當主詞，不能省略。
  • could lead to 表示「可能導致」，語氣比 will lead to 較保守。

【主題例句】

Engineers use materials that can resist corrosion in wet environments.
（工程師會使用能在潮濕環境中抗腐蝕的材料。）

【學習提醒】

形容詞子句要緊接被修飾的名詞，否則讀者可能誤會它修飾哪一個對象。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ Moisture intrusion that could lead to corrosion is dangerous, it damages concrete.

✓ Moisture intrusion that could lead to corrosion is dangerous because it damages concrete.

說明：兩個完整句子不能只用逗號連接，需加入連接詞或改成兩句。

題目：The joints protect bridges from water ______ can damage concrete. A. who B. that C. what D. where

答案：B。說明：先行詞 water 是事物，且關係代名詞在子句中當主詞，所以用 that。

【原因副詞子句】Adverb Clause of Reason 原因副詞子句

【引句 Quote】

Expansion joints are crucial components in bridge engineering because bridges constantly respond to environmental forces that cause their materials to move.

【句型】

S + V ... because + S + V ...

【核心說明】

because 引導原因副詞子句，用來說明主要子句成立的原因。本文先說伸縮縫很重要，再用 because 解釋橋梁會受到環境力量影響，因此需要能吸收位移的設計。

because 的原因語氣直接而清楚，適合回答 why 類問題，也常見於說明文。

【使用規則】

  • because 後面接完整子句，不接單獨名詞。
  • because of 是介係詞片語，後面接名詞或動名詞。
  • because 子句可放句首或句尾；放句首時，通常用逗號隔開主句。
  • 正式寫作中可用 since 或 as 表示較弱或已知的原因。

【主題例句】

Engineers inspect bridges often because small cracks can become serious problems.
（工程師經常檢查橋梁，因為小裂縫可能變成嚴重問題。）

【學習提醒】

because 與 because of 不可混用；because 後面要有主詞與動詞，because of 後面不可直接接完整句。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ The bridge was closed because of the concrete was damaged.

✓ The bridge was closed because the concrete was damaged.

說明：the concrete was damaged 是完整子句，所以前面要用 because，不可用 because of。

題目：The road was repaired ______ heavy traffic had damaged its surface. A. because B. because of C. due D. owing

答案：A。說明：後方是完整子句 heavy traffic had damaged its surface，因此選 because。

折疊四：練文筆食材⌄

① because + S + V（因為……）
→ 適用：清楚說明原因。
例：Expansion joints are necessary because bridges move with temperature changes.

② such as + N（例如……）
→ 適用：列舉具體例子。
例：Bridge materials such as steel and concrete react to heat.

③ without + N/V-ing（若沒有……；沒有做……）
→ 適用：描述缺乏某條件造成的後果。
例：Without proper joints, bridges may crack over time.

④ leading to + N（導致……）
→ 適用：表達結果或後果。
例：Poor drainage may allow water to enter the structure, leading to corrosion.

⑤ making + O + C（使……變得……）
→ 適用：把前句情況轉成結果。
例：Temperature changes create stress, making flexible design essential.

⑥ not only... but also...（不僅……而且……）
→ 適用：強調雙重功能或優點。
例：Expansion joints not only reduce stress but also protect bridges from damage.

⑦ be determined by + N（由……決定）
→ 適用：說明某結果的決定因素。
例：The size of the gap is determined by the bridge's design.

⑧ be proportional to + N（與……成比例）
→ 適用：描述數學或科學關係。
例：The amount of expansion is proportional to the rise in temperature.

⑨ ranging from A to B（範圍從 A 到 B）
→ 適用：呈現數字範圍。
例：The movement may range from a few millimeters to several centimeters.

⑩ depending on + N（取決於……）
→ 適用：說明條件差異。
例：The material choice differs depending on the bridge's location.

⑪ thereby + V-ing（因此……）
→ 適用：正式寫作中表結果。
例：The new coating blocks water, thereby slowing corrosion.

⑫ under varying conditions（在不同條件下）
→ 適用：討論系統在多種情境中的表現。
例：Engineers test bridge parts under varying conditions.
