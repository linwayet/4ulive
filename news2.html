區塊一：英文文章原文

In recent years, a troubling trend known as "teen takeovers" has spread across several major American cities. These events occur when large groups of teenagers gather suddenly at public spaces — such as shopping malls, parks, or beaches — after being organized through social media platforms. In Milwaukee, Wisconsin, for example, police responded to teen takeovers at Bayshore Mall, Mayfair Mall, and Moody Park in early 2026, resulting in over a dozen arrests of individuals between the ages of 12 and 19. Milwaukee authorities even released drone footage of one gathering to warn the public about the dangers these events can cause.

Chicago has experienced similarly alarming incidents. In April 2025, a 14-year-old boy was charged in connection with a shooting that injured a 15-year-old during one such gathering in downtown Chicago. More recently, on Memorial Day 2026, a teen takeover at 57th Street Beach turned chaotic, forcing dozens of police officers to respond and leading to the temporary closure of part of DuSable Lake Shore Drive. Chicago Police and school officials have since sent warning letters to parents about social media messages organizing future events.

These incidents raise serious questions about teenage behavior, parental responsibility, and the power of social media. City leaders in both Milwaukee and Chicago have proposed stricter curfews and expanded police patrols to prevent further disruptions. While most teenagers are not involved in such gatherings, experts stress that open communication between parents and teens remains one of the most effective tools to keep young people safe.

區塊四：文法解析

【文法一】分詞構句（Participial Construction）
【引句 Quote】In Milwaukee... police responded to teen takeovers... resulting in over a dozen arrests.
【句型】S + V..., V-ing...
【核心說明】本句中的 resulting in 為「分詞構句」，用來表示主要子句動作所產生的「結果」。這類構句通常省略了連接詞 (如 and it resulted in)，使句子更簡潔有力。
【使用規則】
  • 主詞需一致：主要子句與分詞片語的主詞必須是同一個。
  • 表結果：放在句尾，通常用現在分詞 (V-ing) 表示主動或必然產生的結果。
  • 逗號分隔：通常在主要子句後加上逗號。
【主題例句】The beach party turned violent, forcing the police to close the road.（海灘派對演變成暴力，迫使警方封閉道路。）
【學習提醒】看到 V-ing 放在句尾且有逗號隔開時，優先判斷它是否在補充說明「結果」或「伴隨動作」。
⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題：
✗ The storm hit the city and causing many deaths.
✓ The storm hit the city, causing many deaths.
說明：已經有連接詞 and 就不用分詞構句；若要用分詞構句則不加 and。
試題：He worked late every night, ________ his health in the end.
(A) ruined (B) ruins (C) ruining (D) to ruin
答案：(C)。
【文法二】形容詞子句（Relative Clauses）：who / which / that
【引句 Quote】Milwaukee authorities... released footage... to warn the public about the dangers these events can cause.
【句型】N + (that/which) + S + V
【核心說明】形容詞子句（關係子句）用來修飾先行詞（名詞）。在引句中，dangers 後面省略了關係代名詞 that/which，因為它在子句中作受詞。
【使用規則】
  • who：指人，which：指物，that：指人或物。
  • 受詞省略：當關係代名詞在子句中擔任動詞或介系詞的受詞時，常可省略。
  • 限定用法：用來限縮名詞的範圍，不可加逗號。
【主題例句】The social media platforms (that) teens use can be very powerful.（青少年使用的社群媒體平台力量可能非常強大。）
【學習提醒】當名詞後面直接跟著另一個主詞和動詞時，中間通常省略了關係代名詞。
⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題：
✗ This is the boy who I saw him yesterday.
✓ This is the boy (who) I saw yesterday.
說明：關係代名詞 who 已代表受詞，後面不可再加 him。
試題：The drone ________ police used was very expensive.
(A) who (B) whose (C) which (D) what
答案：(C)。
【文法三】被動語態（Passive Voice）
【引句 Quote】In April 2025, a 14-year-old boy was charged in connection with a shooting...
【句型】S + be + p.p. (+ by...)
【核心說明】被動語態用來強調「動作的承受者」。當動作的執行者（如：警方）顯而易見或不需特別強調時，常使用被動語態。
【使用規則】
  • 結構：be 動詞（隨時態變化）+ 過去分詞 (p.p.)。
  • 及物動詞：只有及物動詞（有受詞的動詞）才能改為被動語態。
  • 時態一致：被動語態的 be 動詞需符合原句時態。
【主題例句】The beach was closed by the police due to safety concerns.（由於安全考量，海灘被警方封閉了。）
【學習提醒】charge 常用於被動語態 be charged with (被指控...)。
⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題：
✗ The accident was happened last night.
✓ The accident happened last night.
說明：happen 是不及物動詞，不可用被動語態。
試題：The new rules ________ by the city council next month.
(A) will propose (B) will be proposed (C) are proposed (D) were proposed
答案：(B)。

折疊四：練文筆食材
① In recent years, a troubling trend known as [Topic] has spread...
→ 適用：開頭段落引入社會現象。

② [Something] occur when [Condition] after being [V-ed] through [Medium].
→ 適用：說明事件發生的背景與機制。

③ The incident resulted in [Number] arrests of individuals between the ages of...
→ 適用：具體說明事件帶來的社會影響。

④ [Authorities] released [Information] to warn the public about the dangers...
→ 適用：描述官方的應對措施與教育目的。

⑤ The situation turned chaotic, forcing [People] to respond...
→ 適用：描述事態擴大及其產生的壓力。

⑥ These incidents raise serious questions about [Problem A], [Problem B], and [Problem C].
→ 適用：分析問題背後的核心爭議（排比法）。

⑦ [Leaders] have proposed [Solution A] and [Solution B] to prevent further [Negative Outcome].
→ 適用：提出改善現狀的政策性建議。

⑧ While [Group A] is not involved, experts stress that [Action] remains one of the most effective tools.
→ 適用：在結論段落平衡觀點，並給出核心建議。
