Talking in the Sea: Humpback Whale Songs

海中的對話：座頭鯨歌聲的秘密

林威老師與 AI agent 團隊製作

英文文章原文

Original Article

Humpback whales have one of the most complex communication systems in the animal world, and sound is their main tool for intercommunication. They produce long, patterned “songs” as well as short calls, grunts, and cries that can travel for many kilometers through dark ocean water where they often cannot see each other. Scientists have even found that these songs are organized in a way that partly resembles the structure of human language, with small sound units combined into phrases and themes.

These vocal signals serve different purposes in whale society. Male humpbacks sing long, repeated songs mainly during the breeding season, probably to attract females and compete with other males over long distances. At closer range, whales use non‑song calls to coordinate group feeding, keep contact while migrating, and send warnings when there is danger or strong competition. Mothers and calves also “whisper” to each other using very soft, low sounds that help them stay together without drawing the attention of predators.

Communication is not only about sounds; it also includes physical actions. When humpbacks slap the water with their tails or huge pectoral fins, or when they breach and crash back into the sea, these loud surface behaviors can work as social signals to nearby whales. Researchers think such signals help whales join new groups, strengthen social bonds, and organize complex activities like cooperative hunting. Because of this rich system of intercommunication, humpback whales can live, migrate, and survive together in the vast and sometimes dangerous ocean.

文法解析

Grammar Focus

一、one of the most + 最高級 + 複數名詞

【引句 Quote】

Humpback whales have one of the most complex communication systems in the animal world.

【句型】

one of the most + adj. + plural N

【核心說明】

one of the most... 表示「最...之一」，用來把某人事物放進一群頂尖或極端的例子中。因為 one of 後面表示「其中之一」，所以名詞必須用複數。

【使用規則】

  • most 後接形容詞，名詞要用複數。
  • 主詞若是 one，動詞通常用單數。
  • 可用於介紹主題的重要性。

【主題例句】

Whale songs are one of the most fascinating forms of animal communication.
（鯨歌是最迷人的動物溝通形式之一。）

【學習提醒】

常見錯誤是把後面的名詞寫成單數。請記得：one of + 複數名詞，因為它表示從多個例子中取一個。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ one of the most complex system
✓ one of the most complex systems（system 要用複數。）

選擇：This is one of the most useful ___ in the book. (A) lesson (B) lessons (C) learning
答案：B。

二、with + 受詞 + 過去分詞

【引句 Quote】

...with small sound units combined into phrases and themes.

【句型】

with + O + V-ed...

【核心說明】

with + 受詞 + 過去分詞 可表示附帶狀態。若受詞與後面的動作是被動關係，就用過去分詞；本文中 sound units 是「被組合」成 phrases and themes。

【使用規則】

  • 主動關係常用 V-ing，被動關係常用 V-ed。
  • 此結構可放在句尾補充狀態。
  • with 後面先放受詞，再放補語。

【主題例句】

The song was recorded with several calls repeated in a pattern.
（這首鯨歌被錄下時，有幾個叫聲以固定模式重複。）

【學習提醒】

判斷 V-ing 或 V-ed 時，先看 with 後面的名詞和動作之間是主動還是被動。sound units cannot combine themselves，所以用 combined。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ with sound units combining into phrases
✓ with sound units combined into phrases（聲音單位是被組合。）

改錯：The whale was seen with its tail raising above the water.
答案：The whale was seen with its tail raised above the water.

三、while + V-ing 省略句

【引句 Quote】

Whales use non-song calls to keep contact while migrating.

【句型】

while + V-ing

【核心說明】

while + V-ing 是 while + S + be V-ing 的簡化形式，用來表示兩個動作同時發生。本文中 while migrating 表示「在遷徙時」。

【使用規則】

  • 省略前，副詞子句主詞通常要和主要子句主詞相同。
  • while 後接 V-ing，表示同時進行。
  • 若主詞不同，不要任意省略。

【主題例句】

Humpbacks use sound while traveling through dark water.
（座頭鯨穿越黑暗海水時會使用聲音。）

【學習提醒】

while migrating 的主詞是 whales，和主要子句主詞相同，因此可以省略 they are。若主詞不同，就必須寫完整子句。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ While migrating, the sounds help whales keep contact.
✓ While migrating, whales use sounds to keep contact.（遷徙的是 whales，不是 sounds。）

選擇：Whales stay in touch while ___ across the ocean. (A) migrate (B) migrating (C) migrated
答案：B。
