一、英文文章原文

As bee populations around the world continue to decline at an alarming rate, scientists are developing an innovative solution: robotic bees. Recent advances at research institutions like Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) and other U.S. institutions have produced tiny flying robots capable of performing pollination tasks similar to those of real bees. These tiny machines represent an impressive technological achievement. MIT's latest models can hover in the air for over 1,000 seconds and perform complex movements demonstrating the agility needed for successful pollination. The robots are designed to mimic the flight patterns of natural bees, offering potential support to agriculture that is increasingly threatened by pollinator shortages. At present, robotic bees show the most promise in controlled settings such as greenhouses and vertical farms, where natural pollinators often cannot survive or function effectively. These have yielded encouraging outcomes with robotic pollination leading to larger fruits and better crop quality in test environments.

These early successes suggest that the technology could be particularly valuable for indoor farming operations. Nevertheless, significant challenges must be overcome before these robots can be widely used in agriculture. Things like flight stability outdoors, limited battery life, and high production costs remain major obstacles. Scientists are currently working to improve autonomous navigation systems and make the robots more durable for practical farming applications. The transition from laboratory settings to real-world farms will require continued research and development. While robotic bees will probably not completely replace natural pollinators, they could become valuable supplementary tools for maintaining food production as environmental conditions continue to change. This emerging technology represents humanity's creative response to ecological challenges and demonstrates how innovation can address complex problems in sustainable agriculture.

The impact of robotic bees extends beyond immediate agricultural concerns. The development of these robots advances our understanding of artificial intelligence, biomimicry, and robotics technology. Engineers studying bee behavior gain insights that inform broader applications in robotics and automation. As climate change and habitat loss threaten natural bee populations, technological solutions like robotic pollinators become increasingly important. Future improvements may include better power systems, enhanced autonomous navigation, and expanded capabilities beyond pollination. The integration of robotic and biological systems might create hybrid pollination strategies that combine the strengths of both approaches. Investment in this technology signals humanity's commitment to developing sustainable solutions for food security and environmental protection. Robotic bees exemplify how scientific innovation can transform challenges into opportunities for a more resilient future.

四、文法解析

1. As 引導的副詞子句：隨著

【引句 Quote】
As bee populations around the world continue to decline at an alarming rate, scientists are developing an innovative solution.

【句型】
As + S + V, S + V...

【核心說明】
As 可表示「隨著」，用來描述兩件事同時發展或互相影響。這種句型常用於環境、科技、社會趨勢等文章，讓讀者看出背景變化與主要行動之間的關係。

【使用規則】

  • As 子句通常放句首，後面用逗號接主要子句。
  • As 表示「隨著」時，不等於 because 的「因為」。
  • 主句常搭配 become、increase、develop、change 等動詞。
  • 若主詞相同且語意清楚，可改成 With + N + V-ing。

【主題例句】
As pollinator shortages become worse, robotic bees may play a larger role.
（隨著授粉者短缺惡化，機器蜜蜂可能扮演更大的角色。）

【學習提醒】
看到 As 不要只翻成「因為」。請先判斷前後句是否呈現同步變化；若是，就更適合翻成「隨著」。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】
✗ As bee populations decline, so scientists develop solutions.
✓ As bee populations decline, scientists develop solutions.（As 子句後直接接主要子句，不需加 so。）

  1. As climate change gets worse, farmers ______ new tools. (A) needing (B) need (C) to need
  2. 改錯：As technology improves, so robots become safer.

2. 分詞片語修飾名詞

【引句 Quote】
MIT's latest models can hover in the air and perform complex movements demonstrating the agility needed for successful pollination.

【句型】
N + V-ing phrase / N + V-ed phrase

【核心說明】
現在分詞 V-ing 常表示主動或正在進行，過去分詞 V-ed 常表示被動或完成。句中 demonstrating 修飾 movements，needed 修飾 agility，讓一句話能濃縮更多資訊。

【使用規則】

  • V-ing 表示被修飾名詞主動做該動作。
  • V-ed 表示被修飾名詞承受該動作或已完成。
  • 分詞片語通常緊接在被修飾名詞後面。
  • 若修飾關係不清楚，應改用完整形容詞子句。

【主題例句】
Robots carrying tiny sensors can collect useful farming data.
（帶有微型感測器的機器人能收集有用的農業資料。）

【學習提醒】
分詞片語不是獨立句子，不能單獨當完整句。考試中要特別注意它修飾的是哪個名詞。

3. 情態助動詞 + 被動語態

【引句 Quote】
Significant challenges must be overcome before these robots can be widely used in agriculture.

【句型】
S + modal + be + past participle...

【核心說明】
情態助動詞如 must、can、could、may 後面接原形動詞。若要表達「被做」，就使用 be + p.p.，例如 must be overcome 表示「必須被克服」。

【使用規則】

  • 情態助動詞後的 be 必須用原形。
  • 過去分詞表示被動動作，如 used、designed、overcome。
  • must be p.p. 表示必要性；can be p.p. 表示可能性或能力。
  • 若要說明動作者，可加 by + N。

【主題例句】
Better batteries must be developed before robotic bees can work outdoors.
（在機器蜜蜂能於戶外工作前，必須開發出更好的電池。）

【學習提醒】
中文常省略「被」，但英文被動語態不能省略 be。看到 must、can、could 後要確認是否需要接 be + p.p.。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】
✗ The problem must overcome soon.
✓ The problem must be overcome soon.（problem 是被克服，所以要用被動語態。）

  1. These robots can ______ in greenhouses. (A) use (B) be used (C) using
  2. 改錯：More tests should done before the machines are sold.

練文筆食材⌄

① As + S + V, S + V（隨著…，…）
→ 適用：描述趨勢變化與結果
例：As technology improves, farming becomes more efficient.

② be capable of V-ing（有能力做…）
→ 適用：說明人、機器或制度的功能
例：Small robots are capable of collecting useful data.

③ similar to those of...（類似於…的那些）
→ 適用：避免重複前面的名詞
例：The problems in this city are similar to those of other large cities.

④ be designed to V（被設計來…）
→ 適用：介紹工具、系統或政策目的
例：The app is designed to help students review vocabulary.

⑤ offering potential support to...（為…提供潛在支援）
→ 適用：說明附帶效果或可能貢獻
例：Online lessons offer potential support to students in remote areas.

⑥ where + S + V（在那裡…）
→ 適用：補充說明地點特色
例：Greenhouses provide a controlled space where crops can grow safely.

⑦ Nevertheless, S + V（然而，…）
→ 適用：從優點轉向限制
例：Nevertheless, the plan still requires more money and time.

⑧ must be + p.p.（必須被…）
→ 適用：指出必要問題或待解任務
例：Several safety issues must be solved before the product is sold.

⑨ While + S + V, S + V（雖然…，…）
→ 適用：讓步與平衡觀點
例：While AI cannot replace teachers, it can support learning.

⑩ as conditions continue to change（隨著情況持續改變）
→ 適用：連結未來趨勢
例：Students need new skills as society continues to change.

⑪ beyond immediate concerns（超越眼前的關切）
→ 適用：延伸論點層次
例：The policy matters beyond immediate economic concerns.

⑫ gain insights that V（獲得能…的洞見）
→ 適用：描述研究或觀察的價值
例：Scientists gained insights that improved medical treatment.

⑬ may include...（可能包括…）
→ 適用：列舉未來改善方向
例：Future changes may include cleaner energy and safer transport.

⑭ combine the strengths of both approaches（結合兩種方法的優勢）
→ 適用：提出折衷或整合方案
例：The school can combine the strengths of online and face-to-face learning.

⑮ transform challenges into opportunities（把挑戰轉化為機會）
→ 適用：結論句、正向收束
例：Good leaders transform challenges into opportunities for growth.
