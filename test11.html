Part 1 ｜ 今日單字本 10 個

🔴 紅框 = 蔡同學段考錯誤詞 · 同主題同等級補充
1
fit in [fɪt ɪn] 片語動詞 融入；被接受 ⚠ 段考錯題
片語 fit in with sb/sth（與…融洽相處）／ fit into a group（融入群體）／ fit in somewhere（適合某地方）
容易混淆 pop up（突然出現，無計劃地出現）；blend in（混入、不顯眼地融入）；stand out（突出、顯眼，與 fit in 相反）
重點 段考考點：Auggie 懷疑自己是否能在新學校「融入」——環境不友善 → 用 fit in
He doubted whether he would ever fit in at his new school.
（他懷疑自己在新學校是否能融入。）
It took her a few weeks, but she finally fit in with the rest of the team.
（她花了幾個禮拜，但終於和其他團隊成員融合在一起了。）
2
fuel [fjuəl] v. / n. （v.）助長；加劇 ／ （n.）燃料 ⚠ 段考錯題
衍生詞 fuel (n.) 燃料／fuel (v.) 助長、激化／fueled (adj.) 以…為動力的／fuel-efficient (adj.) 省油的
片語 fuel anxiety / fear / tension（助長焦慮／恐懼／緊張）；fuel a debate（激起辯論）；add fuel to the fire（火上加油）
重點 名詞轉動詞類型：fuel、fire、mirror、champion 等字，動詞義與名詞義完全不同，要特別記！
Rising housing prices continue to fuel young people's anxiety.
（不斷上漲的房價持續加劇年輕人的焦慮。）
The media coverage only fueled the public's fear about the disease.
（媒體的報導只是進一步助長了公眾對該疾病的恐懼。）
3
widen [ˈwaɪdən] v. （使）擴大；（使）增寬 ⚠ 段考錯題
衍生詞 wide (adj.) 寬的／widely (adv.) 廣泛地／width (n.) 寬度／widen (v.) 使擴大／widespread (adj.) 廣泛的
片語 widen interest / influence / gap（擴大興趣/影響力/差距）；widen one's horizons（開拓視野）
容易混淆 broaden（拓展，常搭配 horizons/knowledge）；expand（擴張，常搭配 business/market）；extend（延長/延伸，含時間/空間義）
His success has widened worldwide interest in baseball.
（他的成功拓展了全球對棒球的興趣。）
Traveling abroad can greatly widen your perspective on life.
（出國旅行能大大拓展你的人生視野。）
4
establish [ɪˈstæblɪʃ] v. 創立；建立；確立 ⚠ 段考錯題
衍生詞 establishment (n.) 機構；建立／established (adj.) 已確立的；知名的／well-established (adj.) 久負盛名的
片語 establish an organization / company / record（創立組織/公司；創下紀錄）；establish a connection（建立聯繫）；establish one's reputation（確立聲望）
容易混淆 adopt（採納想法/政策；收養小孩）—— 不能搭配「創立組織」；found（創辦，與 establish 近義但更正式）；set up（建立，口語較常用）
He had established an organization to protect children's rights.
（他創立了一個保護兒童權益的組織。）
She worked hard to establish herself as a leading scientist in the field.
（她努力讓自己在該領域確立了頂尖科學家的地位。）
5
scatter [ˈskætɚ] v. / n. （v.）散佈；驅散 ／ （n.）散亂
衍生詞 scattered (adj.) 分散的；零散的／scattering (n.) 散布／scatter-brained (adj.) 粗心健忘的
片語 scatter across / along（散布在…各處）；scatter in all directions（四散）；scattered showers（零星陣雨）
Strong typhoon winds scattered plastic waste along Taiwan's beaches.
（颱風強風將塑膠廢棄物散布在台灣海灘各處。）
The crowd scattered in all directions when the alarm went off.
（警報響起時，人群四散而逃。）
6
temporary [ˈtɛmpəˌrɛrɪ] adj. 暫時的；臨時的
衍生詞 temporarily (adv.) 暫時地／temporariness (n.) 暫時性／temporary (adj.) 暫時的
容易混淆 permanent（永久的，反義）；provisional（臨時的，正式書面用語）；interim（過渡期的，政治/行政用語）
慣用語 a temporary solution（暫時的解決辦法）；temporary measures（臨時措施）；on a temporary basis（以臨時方式）
Some countries made temporary changes to oil policies due to rising global prices.
（由於全球油價上漲，部分國家對石油政策做了暫時性的調整。）
The pain from the injection is only temporary and will pass in minutes.
（注射的疼痛只是暫時的，幾分鐘後就會消失。）
7
inferior [ɪnˈfɪrɪɚ] adj. / n. （adj.）劣等的；低等的 ／ （n.）下屬
衍生詞 inferiority (n.) 劣等；自卑／inferior (adj.) 劣等的／superior (adj.) 優等的（反義）
片語 feel inferior to sb（感到不如某人）；inferior quality（品質低劣的）；inferiority complex（自卑感）
字根 infer-（下方、較低）— inferior（較低的）/ infernal（地獄的）；-or（比較級字尾）— superior（更高的）/ interior（內部的）
Some workers feel inferior in today's AI-driven workplace.
（在當今由 AI 主導的職場中，有些員工感到自身能力不如他人。）
She refused to see herself as inferior to anyone in the company.
（她拒絕認為自己在公司中不如任何人。）
8
marvelous [ˈmɑrvələs] adj. 了不起的；令人驚歎的
衍生詞 marvel (n.) 奇蹟、令人驚歎的事物；(v.) 對…感到驚歎／marvelously (adv.) 了不起地／marvelous (adj.) 了不起的
片語 do a marvelous job（做得非常出色）；marvel at sth（對某事感到驚歎）
The rescue team did a marvelous job saving people trapped after the earthquake.
（救援隊在地震後拯救被困民眾，表現得非常出色。）
We stood in silence and marveled at the beauty of the northern lights.
（我們靜靜站著，對北極光的美景嘆為觀止。）
9
liberty [ˈlɪbɚtɪ] n. 自由；自主權
衍生詞 liberate (v.) 解放／liberation (n.) 解放、自由／liberal (adj.) 自由的；開明的／liberalism (n.) 自由主義
字根 liber-（自由）— liberty（自由）/ liberate（解放）/ liberal（自由的）/ deliver（遞送；解脫）
慣用語 at liberty to do sth（可以自由做某事）；take the liberty of doing（冒昧做…）；civil liberties（公民自由）
Teenagers believe home rules limit their liberty to use smartphones freely.
（青少年認為家規限制了他們自由使用智慧型手機的自主權。）
Iqbal fought to give bonded children the liberty they deserved.
（Iqbal 為讓那些被束縛的孩子獲得應有的自由而奮鬥。）
10
persuasive [pɚˈswesɪv] adj. 有說服力的；能使人信服的
衍生詞 persuade (v.) 說服／persuasion (n.) 說服；勸說／persuasively (adv.) 有說服力地／unpersuasive (adj.) 缺乏說服力的
片語 persuade sb to do sth（說服某人做…）；persuade sb of sth（說服某人相信…）；a persuasive argument（有說服力的論點）
容易混淆 convince（使確信，著重「讓人改變想法」）；persuade（說服，著重「勸說使人採取行動」）
His speeches were so persuasive that he was invited to speak in other countries.
（他的演講極具說服力，因此受邀前往其他國家演講。）
She made a persuasive case for changing the company's environmental policy.
（她為改變公司的環保政策提出了極具說服力的論據。）

Part 2 ｜ 文法重點 3 個

蔡同學最容易進步的三個考點 · 關鍵字粗體標示

文法點 1｜後接動名詞的動詞（V + V-ing）

段考考點 (18)：No one minded __(18)__ near him anymore.
「No one minded being near him anymore, and for the first time, he felt at ease.」
S + V（特定動詞）+ V-ing ...
核心說明
英文中有一群動詞，後面只能接動名詞（V-ing），不能接不定詞（to V）或原形動詞（bare V）。mind 就是其中之一，意思是「介意；反對做…」。記憶口訣：這些動詞多半與「已發生、持續中的動作」有關。
必背「只接 V-ing」動詞清單
  • mind（介意）— Do you mind waiting?
  • enjoy（享受）— She enjoys reading novels.
  • avoid（避免）— He avoided making eye contact.
  • finish（完成）— They finished building the house.
  • consider（考慮）— Are you considering applying?
  • give up（放棄）— She gave up trying.
  • keep（持續）— Keep practicing every day.
  • suggest（建議）— He suggested going early.
主題例句
No one minded sitting next to Auggie anymore.
（再也沒有人介意坐在 Auggie 旁邊了。）

Iqbal avoided drawing attention to himself when he first escaped.
（Iqbal 剛逃脫時，盡量避免引起別人注意。）
學習提醒
注意：mind 後若接原形動詞（be）是常見錯誤！選項中若出現 be，通常是陷阱。另外，stop + V-ing 表示「停止做…」；stop + to V 表示「停下來去做另一件事」，兩者意思完全不同！
✗ No one minded be near him. （❌ 錯誤：mind 後不接原形）
✓ No one minded being near him. （✅ 正確：mind + V-ing）

文法點 2｜other 的四種用法辨別

段考考點 (20)：...and __(20)__ students were left in shock.
「Malala fell to the ground, and the other students were left in shock.」
other + N ／ another ／ the other(s) ／ others
四種用法對比表
  • another（再一個）— 泛指，單數，可數。I'd like another cup of tea. （再來一杯）
  • other + N（其他的…）— 泛指，後加複數名詞。Other students raised their hands. （其他學生…）
  • others（其他人/事）— 泛指，複數代名詞，後不加名詞。Some passed; others failed. （有些人過了；其他人沒有）
  • the other(s)（那些特定的其他人/事）— 特指，範圍已確定。Of the two, one left; the other stayed. ／ All students on the bus: Malala was shot, and the other students were shocked.
段考分析
車上有一群特定的學生，排除 Malala 之後，其餘都是「那些其他的學生」，範圍確定 → 用 the other students。若用 others students，文法錯誤（others 後不加名詞）；若用 other students，語意變成泛指「其他學生」，失去特指的邏輯。
✗ Others students were left in shock. （❌ others 後不接名詞）
✓ The other students were left in shock. （✅ 特指同一車廂裡的其他學生）

文法點 3｜連接詞語意辨別：while / when / until / as

段考考點 (16)：Everything seemed fine __(16)__ a group suddenly appeared.
「Everything seemed to be going well while a group of seventh-graders suddenly shone a flashlight into his face.」
主句 + while / when / until / as + 副詞子句
四個連接詞語意對比
  • while（當…的同時；突然…）— 強調「進行中被打斷」或「對比」。Everything was fine while chaos suddenly broke out. ✔
  • when（當…時）— 某一時間點發生的動作，較中性。I was studying when she called. ✔
  • until（直到…為止）— 表示動作持續到某時間點才停止。He waited until she arrived. ✔
  • as（隨著；一邊…一邊）— 表示兩動作同時進行或漸進變化。Prices rose as demand increased. ✔
段考分析
「一切順利 → 突然有人出現搗亂」，是進行中被打斷的情境。while 在這裡表達「正當一切順利進行時，突然發生了另一件事」，帶有強烈的轉折與突發感。until 的邏輯是「一直順利，直到某個時間點才停止」，缺少突發的語感，不適合。
✗ Everything went well until suddenly someone appeared. （❌ until 不能搭配 suddenly 的突發感）
✓ Everything went well while someone suddenly appeared. （✅ while 強調「進行中被打斷」）

Part 3 ｜ 閱讀測驗

學測長度 × 1/2 · 主題：社會融入與個人成長 · 5 題閱讀理解

Maya had always been the new kid. By the time she entered high school, she had moved five times, making it difficult to fit in anywhere. At her new school in Tainan, she noticed that her classmates formed tight groups during lunch. Maya often sat alone, and she even doubted whether she would ever become part of the community.

However, Maya refused to give up. She established a small English conversation club, hoping to connect with students who shared her interest in languages. At first, only two students joined. Yet, their early conversations were so persuasive about the value of the club that word quickly spread. Within a month, more than thirty students had signed up.

The club's success not only helped Maya socially but also widened her influence across the school. Teachers began inviting her to help with bilingual events. A local newspaper even wrote a short article about the club, calling it a "marvelous example of student-led initiative."

Looking back, Maya believes that feelings of being inferior to others are natural when entering a new environment. What matters is finding a way to contribute rather than simply trying to blend in. She now gives short talks at school, encouraging others: "You don't have to wait to be accepted. Create something, and the right people will find you."

1 Why did Maya find it difficult to fit in when she entered high school?
(A) She was not able to speak Chinese well enough.
(B) She had moved so many times that building friendships was hard.
(C) She did not enjoy spending time with other students.
(D) Her school did not welcome students from other cities.
2 What did Maya do in order to connect with her classmates?
(A) She asked teachers to introduce her to popular students.
(B) She started an English conversation club at her school.
(C) She wrote a newspaper article about her experience.
(D) She changed her behavior to match what others expected.
3 The word "persuasive" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to ______.
(A) confusing
(B) convincing
(C) temporary
(D) scattered
4 According to the passage, what happened after the club became popular?
(A) Maya was asked to stop running the club.
(B) Maya transferred to a larger school in Taipei.
(C) Teachers invited Maya to help with bilingual events.
(D) The club was shut down because it grew too large.
5 What is the main message of this passage?
(A) Moving to a new city is always a negative experience for students.
(B) Creating something meaningful is a better way to belong than waiting to be accepted.
(C) English clubs are the most effective way to make friends in school.
(D) Students should focus only on academic performance to gain respect.
📋 點此查看答案 & 解析  ▼
第1題：(B)
文章第一段說明 Maya 曾搬家五次（moved five times），導致她難以在任何地方融入（fit in anywhere）。選項(B)直接呼應此原因。
第2題：(B)
第二段明確指出 Maya 創立了英文會話社（established a small English conversation club），選項(B)正確。
第3題：(B)
persuasive 意為「有說服力的」，最接近 convincing（令人信服的）。temporary（暫時的）和 scattered（零散的）語意完全不符。
第4題：(C)
第三段提到「Teachers began inviting her to help with bilingual events」，選項(C)直接對應。
第5題：(B)
文章末段 Maya 的話點出主旨：「You don't have to wait to be accepted. Create something, and the right people will find you.」—— 主動創造比等待被接受更有效，對應選項(B)。

Part 4 ｜ 單字單選複習 5 題

點選後查看答案 & 解析 · 手機橫向顯示效果更佳
第 1 題
The new student found it hard to            with the rest of the class because most friendships had already been formed.
(A) pop up
(B) fit in
(C) stand out
(D) break down
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
答案：(B) fit in（融入）
語境是新同學難以「融入」已有友誼的班級，fit in（融入環境、被群體接受）是唯一符合的答案。pop up（突然出現）、stand out（突出）、break down（崩潰；拋錨）均不符合語意。
第 2 題
The government's poor handling of the crisis only            public anger rather than reducing it.
(A) pretended
(B) marveled
(C) fueled
(D) scattered
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
答案：(C) fueled（助長）
fuel（v.）意指「助長、激化」，搭配 anger / tension / debate 非常自然。"rather than reducing it" 也提示需要一個「使憤怒增加」的動詞。pretended（假裝）、marveled（驚歎）、scattered（驅散）均不符。
第 3 題
The project team            a new system for tracking student progress, which later became a model for other schools.
(A) adopted
(B) established
(C) considered
(D) scattered
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
答案：(B) established（建立、創立）
establish a system（建立一套系統）是固定搭配。adopt（採納）也可搭配 system，但語意是「採用已有的系統」，而這裡強調的是「創立新系統」，因此 established 更精準。
第 4 題
The international exchange program has greatly            students' understanding of global cultures and issues.
(A) punched
(B) fueled
(C) widened
(D) pretended
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
答案：(C) widened（擴大）
widen + understanding / knowledge / perspective / gap 是固定搭配，表示「擴大理解的範圍」。punched（打）、fueled（助長，通常搭配負面情緒）、pretended（假裝）均不符語境。
第 5 題
No one            taking extra time to help the team, because everyone understood how important the project was.
(A) minded taking
(B) minded to take
(C) minded take
(D) mind taking
點此查看答案 & 解析 ▼
答案：(A) minded taking（介意花時間…）
mind 是「只接 V-ing」的動詞，不接 to V 或原形動詞。主詞是 No one（沒有人），動詞用過去式 minded（與前後文時態一致），因此 (A) minded taking 是唯一正確選項。(D) mind taking 時態錯誤。

📌 附錄四：練文筆食材 — 今日寫作句型▼
①
doubt whether + S + V（懷疑是否…）
→ 適用：表達不確定、自我懷疑
She doubted whether she would ever fit in with the new team.
（她懷疑自己是否能夠融入新團隊。）
②
not only... but also...（不只…而且…）
→ 適用：強調雙重效果
The program not only widened her knowledge but also fueled her passion for learning.
（這個計劃不只拓展了她的知識，也激發了她對學習的熱情。）
③
instead of + V-ing（不是…而是…）
→ 適用：說明選擇了更好的做法
Instead of waiting to be accepted, she established a club of her own.
（她沒有等待被接受，而是自己創立了一個社團。）
④
within + 時間（在…時間之內）
→ 適用：強調速度快
Within a month, more than thirty students had joined the club.
（在一個月內，超過三十名學生加入了社團。）
⑤
S + V, calling it "..."（分詞構句，稱之為…）
→ 適用：引述他人評語
A local newspaper wrote an article about the club, calling it a marvelous example of student leadership.
（當地報紙為該社團寫了一篇文章，稱之為學生領導力的絕佳範例。）
