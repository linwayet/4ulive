📚 區塊一｜10 個單字（3 個新字＋7 個熟字複習）
① boost [bust]
v. 增強；推升　n. 提振；推動力　⚠️ 你第 11 題選錯
衍生詞 booster n. 助推器；強化劑｜boosted adj. 受推動的
片語 boost morale 提振士氣｜give a boost to 對⋯有幫助
容易混淆 boast v. 自誇（學測第 11 題的 C 選項，注意拼字差一個字母！）

A balanced diet rich in vitamins helps boost your immune system.

富含維他命的均衡飲食有助於提升你的免疫系統。

The coach's speech boosted the team's confidence before the game.

教練的演講在比賽前提升了球隊的信心。

網路廣告大幅推升了這家店的假日銷售。

② deception [dɪˋsɛpʃən]
n. 欺騙；騙術　⚠️ 你第 13 題選錯

字根 cept-「拿、取」；同字根：accept（接受）、except（除了）、concept（概念）
容易混淆 distinction n. 區別（學測第 13 題 D 選項，意思完全不同）

她對於他隱瞞真名一事感到受傷。

這產品的包裝看似簡單實則非常有效。

③ resilience [rɪˋzɪlɪəns]

📐 區塊二｜3 個你最容易進步的文法點

① 分詞構句：leave + O + V-ing / Vpp（學測第 33 題）

當動詞 leave 後面跟受詞時，受詞補語要用「現在分詞」表示「使⋯持續處於某狀態」。看受詞是主動感覺或被動感覺來決定。

leave + sb. + feeling + adj.／V-ing （讓某人感到⋯）
leave + sb. + V-pp （讓某人被⋯）

Empathy sometimes leaves you feeling drained.

同理心有時讓你感到精疲力盡。

（you 是主動感覺到 drained 這個狀態，所以用 V-ing 的 feeling）

The news left her shocked.

這個消息讓她感到震驚。

（she 是被消息「震驚」，用 Vpp）

⚠️ 學測第 33 題你選了 (A) feel drained，但前面是 leaves you，受詞補語要用 V-ing，正確是 (B) feeling drained。

② 與過去事實相反的假設：If S + had Vpp, S + would have Vpp（學測第 38 題）

表達「如果當時⋯就不會⋯」這種「對過去的後悔或假設」。If 子句用過去完成式，主要子句用 would / could / should have + Vpp。

If + S + had Vpp, S + would/could/should + have Vpp

If these professionals had known more about self-care years ago, they would not have suffered so deeply.

如果這些專業人員多年前能更了解自我照護，他們就不會受這麼深的苦。

If you had studied harder, you would have passed the test.

如果你當時更用功，你就會通過考試了。

⚠️ 學測第 38 題正確答案是 (B) had known。記得「過去事實相反」一定用 had Vpp，不能用 knew（那是與現在事實相反）。

③ 過去分詞構句：Vpp..., S + V（學測第 56 題）

當主要子句的主詞「被」分詞所表達的動作影響時，分詞構句用過去分詞 (Vpp) 開頭，等於 (Being) Vpp。

Vpp + ..., S + V （= Because/As S + be Vpp, S + V）

Many parents made the choice to part with their children, convinced it was the only way.

許多父母做出與孩子分離的決定，他們深信這是唯一的辦法。

（parents 是被說服 → 用 convinced，等於 being convinced）

Deeply disturbed by what she saw, Irena joined the resistance.

深受所見之事困擾，Irena 加入了反抗運動。

（Irena 是被「所見之事」困擾 → Vpp）

⚠️ 學測第 56 題你選了 (A) convincing（主動「使人信服」），但句子要的是 parents 被「說服」，正解 (D) convinced。判斷主被動是關鍵！

📖 區塊三｜閱讀測驗（學測一半長度，約 180 字）

The Quiet Strength of Saying "No"

In an age where productivity is celebrated, many young professionals feel drained by the constant pressure to please others. Psychologists now emphasize that learning to say "no" is not a sign of weakness, but a form of emotional resilience. Without firm boundaries, the endless absorption of other people's demands can exceed a person's emotional capacity.

Therapist Dr. Lisa Chen argues that healthy adults must maintain a clear emotional distance from work. "If my clients had known this earlier in their careers," she notes, "many would not have suffered burnout." She also warns against deception — pretending to be fine when you are not. Real strength, she insists, requires honesty about your limits.

Making time for rest can boost productivity in the long run. Even small breaks leave the brain feeling refreshed. The long-term prospects for your career depend less on how much you do, and more on whether you can sustain the energy to keep doing it.

1. What is the main idea of the passage?

(A) Young workers should always say yes to assignments.

(B) Saying "no" can help protect emotional health.

(C) Therapists no longer believe in setting boundaries.

(D) Productivity is more important than rest.

2. According to Dr. Chen, what is a form of "deception" that harms people?

(A) Hiding your true emotional limits from yourself.

(B) Telling clients about their illnesses.

(C) Working harder than your colleagues.

(D) Refusing to set career goals.

3. The phrase "feeling refreshed" in the last paragraph is closest in meaning to ____.

(A) emotionally drained　(B) physically exhausted

(C) mentally restored　(D) entirely confused

📝 點此查看閱讀答案＆解析 ▼

1. (B) 主旨題。整篇都在說「設界線、學會說 no」可保護情緒健康。

2. (A) 細節題。第二段 Dr. Chen 警告 "pretending to be fine when you are not" → 對自己隱瞞真實情緒極限。

3. (C) 詞義題。refreshed 是「重新煥發」，相當於 mentally restored（心智恢復）。

✏️ 區塊四｜5 題單字單選複習

1. A balanced diet rich in vitamins is essential to ______ your immune system against seasonal flu.

(A) attempt　(B) escape　(C) boast　(D) boost

2. The camouflage of some species such as octopus is a perfect example of ______ in the natural world.

(A) deception　(B) limitation　(C) temptation　(D) distinction

3. Emotional ______ is what helps healthcare workers recover from daily stress without burning out.

(A) resilience　(B) reluctance　(C) reduction　(D) recreation

4. After working three night shifts in a row, the nurse felt completely ______.

(A) drained　(B) drowned　(C) drowsy　(D) drifted

5. Psychologists recommend that everyone ______ a healthy emotional distance to avoid burnout.

(A) maintains　(B) maintained　(C) maintain　(D) maintaining

📝 點此查看單字測驗答案＆解析 ▼

1. (D) boost 增強免疫系統 = boost the immune system。(C) boast 是自誇，拼字相近但意思完全不同！學測原題你選錯了，要記住。

2. (A) deception 章魚偽裝 = 欺敵 = deception。學測原題你選 (D) distinction（區別）是字義混淆。

3. (A) resilience 韌性、復原力。是今天主題的核心字。

4. (A) drained feel drained = 感到精疲力盡。注意 drained 是過去分詞當形容詞用。

5. (C) maintain 虛擬語氣！recommend that S + (should) 原形動詞。同類動詞還有：suggest, insist, demand, require, propose, urge。
