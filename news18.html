From Rejection to Global Stardom

從被拒絕到全球走紅：EJ 的音樂逆轉人生

林威老師與 AI agent 團隊製作

英文文章原文

Original Article

From rejection to global stardom, the inspiring journey of EJ. If you have been following pop culture or watching Netflix recently, you've almost certainly heard the song Golden. It is the powerful anthem from the 2025 animated movie K-pop Demon Hunters, sung by the character Rumi and her K-pop girl group Huntrix. However, the real voice behind Rumi belongs to a singer named Kim Eun-jae, better known as EJ.

Her true story is every bit as inspiring as the film that brought her fame. EJ's journey began when she made a life-changing decision at the age of 11. The young girl from Seoul joined SM Entertainment, one of Korea's biggest K-pop production companies, as a trainee with the dream of debuting in a girl group. There, she spent 10 years practicing singing and dancing for long hours as she waited for her moment to take the stage.

She did not come true, and she was told she was too old to debut. EJ faced deep disappointment after a decade of hard work, so she made the tough decision to step away from the idle path. Instead, she chose to continue her education in New York, where she learned about songwriting and music production.

The choice to continue her studies helped EJ discover that she could express herself more freely through songwriting. She began developing her skills as a writer and composer, co-writing hit songs for major K-pop groups such as Red Velvet and Aespa. Everything changed with K-pop demon hunters. The film's producers needed a singing voice for Rumi, and when they heard EJ's demo tapes, they realized her voice was perfect.

Interestingly, EJ's life closely paralleled that of her character, Rumi. Both had faced difficult challenges and had to fight for their place in the world. Fans fell in love not only with Rumi, but also with EJ. The success of the film's soundtrack launched EJ into the spotlight. Golden topped global charts and earned five Grammy nominations, while fans around the world celebrated the song and the singer behind it.

EJ, who had experienced the frustration of rejection years earlier, was performing on stage and in television shows. EJ's story shows that when one door closes, another often opens, but only if you keep trying. Even if you do not achieve success in the way you originally planned, hard work can still lead you to something magical, and perhaps even bigger than you imagined.

8句精讀

8 Key Sentence Analyses

①現在完成進行式

If you have been following pop culture or watching Netflix recently, you've almost certainly heard the song Golden.
如果你最近有在關注流行文化或看 Netflix，你幾乎一定聽過〈Golden〉這首歌。
文法解析：句首 If 引導條件副詞子句，使用現在完成進行式 have been following，強調「從過去延續到最近」的行為。主句 you've heard 是現在完成式，表示過去經驗與現在認知有關。整句用 recently 和 almost certainly 加強「近期熱門到很難沒聽過」的語氣。
pop culture 流行文化almost certainly 幾乎肯定

②過去分詞片語

It is the powerful anthem from the 2025 animated movie K-pop Demon Hunters, sung by the character Rumi and her K-pop girl group Huntrix.
它是 2025 年動畫電影《K-pop Demon Hunters》中強而有力的主題歌，由角色 Rumi 和她的 K-pop 女團 Huntrix 演唱。
文法解析：sung by... 是過去分詞片語，補充說明 anthem「由誰演唱」。過去分詞帶有被動意義，相當於 which is sung by...。句中 from the 2025 animated movie 是介系詞片語，說明歌曲來源。
anthem 主題歌；頌歌animated movie 動畫電影

③關係子句與同位語

The young girl from Seoul joined SM Entertainment, one of Korea's biggest K-pop production companies, as a trainee with the dream of debuting in a girl group.
這位來自首爾的年輕女孩加入了韓國最大 K-pop 製作公司之一 SM Entertainment，成為練習生，夢想在女團出道。
文法解析：one of Korea's biggest K-pop production companies 是同位語，補充解釋 SM Entertainment 的身分。as a trainee 表示「以練習生的身分」。with the dream of V-ing 表示「懷著做某事的夢想」，of 是介係詞，所以後面接 debuting。
trainee 練習生debut in a group 在團體出道

④時間副詞子句

There, she spent 10 years practicing singing and dancing for long hours as she waited for her moment to take the stage.
在那裡，她花了十年長時間練習唱歌和跳舞，等待自己登上舞台的時刻。
文法解析：spend + 時間 + V-ing 是常考句型，表示「花時間做某事」。as she waited... 是時間副詞子句，表示兩件事同時發生。to take the stage 中的 to 是不定詞，修飾 moment，表示「登台的時刻」。
take the stage 登上舞台for long hours 長時間地

⑤so 連接結果

EJ faced deep disappointment after a decade of hard work, so she made the tough decision to step away from the idle path.
經過十年的努力後，EJ 面對深刻的失望，因此她做出艱難決定，離開偶像之路。
文法解析：so 連接前後兩個完整子句，表示結果。after a decade of hard work 是時間介系詞片語，交代失望發生的背景。to step away from... 是不定詞片語，說明 decision 的內容。
disappointment 失望a decade of 十年的step away from 離開

⑥受詞子句

The choice to continue her studies helped EJ discover that she could express herself more freely through songwriting.
繼續求學這個選擇幫助 EJ 發現，她能透過寫歌更自由地表達自己。
文法解析：The choice to continue her studies 是主詞，其中 to continue 是不定詞修飾 choice。helped EJ discover 是 help + O + 原形動詞的結構。that she could express... 是名詞子句，作 discover 的受詞。
express herself 表達自己through songwriting 透過寫歌

⑦對等連接詞

Fans fell in love not only with Rumi, but also with EJ.
粉絲不只愛上了 Rumi，也愛上了 EJ。
文法解析：not only... but also... 連接兩個平行結構，這裡連接 with Rumi 和 with EJ。使用此句型時，前後詞性或結構要一致。這句凸顯觀眾從角色延伸到真實歌手的喜愛。
fall in love with 愛上not only... but also... 不只……而且……

⑧讓步副詞子句

Even if you do not achieve success in the way you originally planned, hard work can still lead you to something magical, and perhaps even bigger than you imagined.
即使你沒有以原本計畫的方式成功，努力仍然可能帶你走向某種奇妙的事物，也許甚至比你想像的更大。
文法解析：Even if 引導讓步副詞子句，表示「即使」。in the way you originally planned 中 the way 後面省略 in which，表示「以……的方式」。lead + O + to + N 表示「帶領某人走向某事」，to 是介係詞，所以後面接名詞 something。
achieve success 獲得成功originally planned 原本計畫lead... to... 帶領……走向……

文法解析

Grammar Analysis

1. 現在完成進行式

【引句 Quote】

If you have been following pop culture or watching Netflix recently, you've almost certainly heard the song Golden.

【句型】

S + have / has been + V-ing + ...

【核心說明】

現在完成進行式用來表示某動作從過去開始，持續到現在或最近，並強調「過程」或「持續性」。文章用 have been following 讓讀者感覺這股流行熱潮不是單一事件，而是最近一段時間都在發酵。

【使用規則】

  • 常搭配 recently、lately、for、since 等時間副詞。
  • 強調動作仍在進行，或剛剛才停止但影響仍在。
  • 不可用於通常不表示進行的狀態動詞，例如 know、believe、belong。
  • 若重點是「完成結果」，常改用現在完成式。

【主題例句】

Fans have been streaming Golden every day since the movie came out.
（自從電影上映後，粉絲每天都在串流播放〈Golden〉。）

【學習提醒】

現在完成進行式不是單純的「現在正在做」，而是把過去到現在的時間拉成一條線。考題若出現 for three years 或 lately，要特別留意此句型。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ She is practicing singing for ten years.

✓ She has been practicing singing for ten years.（for ten years 表示從過去延續到現在。）

Golden ________ popular around the world recently. (A) has becoming (B) has been becoming (C) is become
答案：B

2. 關係副詞 where 引導形容詞子句

【引句 Quote】

Instead, she chose to continue her education in New York, where she learned about songwriting and music production.

【句型】

place + where + S + V ...

【核心說明】

where 引導形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的地點名詞。這裡 where she learned... 補充說明 New York 對 EJ 的意義：它不是單純地點，而是她學習寫歌與音樂製作的轉折點。

【使用規則】

  • 先行詞必須是地點，例如 city、school、place、country。
  • where 可改寫為 in which，但語氣較正式。
  • 若關係子句缺少受詞，不用 where，應用 which/that。
  • 逗號後的 where 子句通常補充額外資訊。

【主題例句】

EJ moved to New York, where she found a new path in music.
（EJ 搬到紐約，在那裡找到了音樂的新道路。）

【學習提醒】

判斷 where 或 which 的關鍵是看子句是否已經有完整主詞與動詞結構。若子句中缺「地點副詞」，用 where；若缺「受詞」，用 which 或 that。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ New York, which she learned songwriting, changed her life.

✓ New York, where she learned songwriting, changed her life.（子句需要地點副詞。）

This is the studio ________ EJ recorded her demo tapes. (A) where (B) which (C) who
答案：A

3. even if 引導讓步副詞子句

【引句 Quote】

Even if you do not achieve success in the way you originally planned, hard work can still lead you to something magical.

【句型】

Even if + S + V ..., S + still + V ...

【核心說明】

even if 表示「即使」，用來承認某種不理想情況仍可能發生，但主句結果依然成立。文章用這個句型把 EJ 的故事提升成給讀者的提醒：成功可能不照原計畫來，但努力仍可能打開新的門。

【使用規則】

  • even if 後接完整子句，不可只接名詞。
  • 主句常搭配 still，強調結果仍然成立。
  • even if 偏假設；even though 偏已發生的事實。
  • 若 even if 子句在句首，後面通常加逗號。

【主題例句】

Even if EJ did not debut as an idol, she still found her voice as a songwriter.
（即使 EJ 沒有以偶像身分出道，她仍以詞曲創作者身分找到自己的聲音。）

【學習提醒】

even if 後面必須有主詞和動詞。若想接名詞，可以改用 despite 或 in spite of，但後面要接名詞或 V-ing。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ Even if failure, she kept trying.

✓ Even if she failed, she kept trying.（even if 後面要接子句。）

________ she was rejected, EJ continued to build a career in music. (A) Even if (B) Because of (C) In order to
答案：A

練文筆食材▼

① From A to B（從 A 到 B）
→ 適用：描述轉變或成長弧線
例：From failure to confidence, the student slowly found his direction.

② If you have been V-ing...（如果你最近一直……）
→ 適用：連結讀者近期經驗
例：If you have been preparing for exams, you know how important time management is.

③ every bit as + adj. + as...（和……一樣……）
→ 適用：強調兩者同樣具備某特質
例：Her real story is every bit as moving as the movie.

④ with the dream of V-ing（懷著……的夢想）
→ 適用：描述目標與動機
例：He moved to Taipei with the dream of becoming a designer.

⑤ spend + 時間 + V-ing（花時間做……）
→ 適用：描述努力過程
例：She spent years improving her English writing.

⑥ be told + S + V（被告知……）
→ 適用：描述外界評價或限制
例：He was told he was not ready, but he kept training.

⑦ make the tough decision to V（做出艱難決定去……）
→ 適用：寫人生選擇或轉折
例：She made the tough decision to leave her comfort zone.

⑧ where + S + V（在那裡……）
→ 適用：補充地點的功能或意義
例：He entered a new school, where he discovered his love for science.

⑨ help + O + V（幫助某人做……）
→ 適用：說明原因帶來的能力提升
例：Reading every day helps students build vocabulary naturally.

⑩ not only... but also...（不只……而且……）
→ 適用：強調雙重影響
例：Music not only comforts people but also gives them courage.

⑪ launch + O + into the spotlight（使……成為焦點）

例：The competition launched the young pianist into the spotlight.

⑫ Even if + S + V（即使……）

例：Even if the result is not perfect, the effort will still matter.

⑬ in the way + S + V（以……的方式）
→ 適用：討論成功、學習或生活方式

例：The project became bigger than we had imagined.
