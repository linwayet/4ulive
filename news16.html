The Surprising Origin of Fanta

芬達汽水的意外誕生故事

英文文章原文

When it comes to orange soda, what pops out in your mind?

Fanta will probably be the answer. However, this colorful and fruity drink usually associated with energy and youngsters today actually has a humble start.

In the winter of 1940, a whole year after WWII broke out, Max Keith, head of Coca-Cola's German subsidiary, sat in his small office and stared at an empty row of syrup barrels. The war had cut off the familiar shipments of ingredients. The sign outside still read Coca-Cola, but inside the plant the machines hissed and the shelves were bare.

Keith refused to shut the doors. He gathered his chemist team and gave them a single, strange instruction: use your imagination. With scarce supplies, imagination would have to be the new raw material. They scoured what was left in the region but only found apple pomace from pressing, whey from dairy farms, and beet leftovers — odd components no one would have thought to blend into a soda. Mixed and tested dozens of times, their "invention" tasted rough and curious at first but then turned to be unexpectedly bright. One afternoon, someone in the team screamed aloud: Fantasie — imagination in German. It stuck; shortened for the label, it became Fanta.

At first Fanta rolled out quietly in wartime Germany where Coca-Cola could no longer be made. It wasn't born of market research but of shortages and stubbornness — a drink invented to keep a factory running and people smiling in hard times. When the war ended, Coca-Cola returned to rebuild its global business. The little soda called Fanta stayed with the company. Since then, it has taken on new life with a range of fruit flavors developed later and expanded its playful imagination internationally as its name suggests.

文法解析

①過去分詞片語修飾名詞

【引句 Quote】

this colorful and fruity drink usually associated with energy and youngsters

【句型】

N + p.p. phrase

【核心說明】

過去分詞片語放在名詞後面時，通常帶有「被動」或「已完成」的意思。本句中 drink 是「被人們聯想到」energy and youngsters，所以使用 associated with。

這種結構常用來把形容詞子句縮短，讓句子更緊湊：the drink that is associated with... 可以簡化成 the drink associated with...。

【使用規則】

  • 被修飾名詞和分詞之間通常有被動關係。
  • 可由「關係代名詞 + be 動詞 + p.p.」簡化而來。
  • 若動作是主動進行，通常改用 V-ing。
  • 分詞片語太長時，多放在名詞後面。

【主題例句】

The soda developed during wartime later became famous worldwide.
（這款在戰時發展出的汽水後來聞名全球。）

【學習提醒】

不要看到 V-ed 就一定翻成過去式。放在名詞後面時，它常是過去分詞修飾語，重點是「被……的」。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ The drink associating with energy became popular.
✓ The drink associated with energy became popular.（drink 是「被聯想到」，用過去分詞。）

試題：The product ________ (create) during the crisis became a global success.
答案：created

②關係副詞 where 引導形容詞子句

【引句 Quote】

Fanta rolled out quietly in wartime Germany where Coca-Cola could no longer be made.

【句型】

place + where + S + V

【核心說明】

where 引導形容詞子句，用來修飾地點名詞。本句中的 where 修飾 wartime Germany，說明那是一個「可口可樂無法再被製造」的地方與時代背景。

where 可改寫成 in which，語氣較正式；例如 Germany where... 等於 Germany in which...。

【使用規則】

  • where 前面通常是 place、city、country、factory 等地點名詞。
  • where 在子句中等於「介系詞 + which」。
  • 若關係詞在子句中當主詞或受詞，不可用 where，要用 which / that。
  • where 子句緊接在它修飾的地點名詞後面。

【主題例句】

The factory where Fanta was first made faced serious shortages.
（芬達最初被製造的工廠面臨嚴重短缺。）

【學習提醒】

不要只因為先行詞是地點就一定用 where。若後面子句缺主詞或受詞，要用 which / that；若子句本身完整、只缺地點副詞，才用 where。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ This is the factory where made Fanta.
✓ This is the factory where Fanta was made.（where 後面要接完整子句。）

試題：This is the office ________ Max Keith worked during the war.
答案：where / in which

③過去完成式表示更早發生的過去

【引句 Quote】

The war had cut off the familiar shipments of ingredients.

【句型】

S + had + p.p. + O

【核心說明】

過去完成式用來表示「在另一個過去時間之前已經發生」的動作。本段先描寫 Keith 坐在辦公室的過去場景，再用 had cut off 回頭交代更早發生的供應中斷。

它常用在敘事文章中，幫助讀者分清楚事件先後順序：不是戰爭當下才切斷供應，而是在那個場景之前已經造成結果。

【使用規則】

  • 句型為 had + 過去分詞。
  • 常與另一個過去式動作一起出現，用來表示較早的動作。
  • 若句中已有 before / after 且順序清楚，有時可用簡單過去式。
  • 過去完成式不可用來表示單純現在以前的經驗，那通常是現在完成式。

【主題例句】

By the time Keith checked the barrels, the usual supplies had disappeared.
（當 Keith 檢查桶子時，平常的供應已經消失了。）

【學習提醒】

中文常只說「已經」，但英文要看時間軸。如果參考點是過去，就用過去完成式；如果參考點是現在，就用現在完成式。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ The war has cut off the shipments before Keith saw the empty barrels.
✓ The war had cut off the shipments before Keith saw the empty barrels.（參考點是過去的 saw。）

試題：When the team arrived, the factory ________ (run out of) syrup.
答案：had run out of

練文筆食材⌄
① When it comes to + N（談到……）
→ 適用：文章開頭引出主題。
例：When it comes to soft drinks, branding often shapes our choices.
② be associated with + N（與……有關）
→ 適用：描述品牌、人物、事件給人的印象。
例：This logo is closely associated with youth and energy.
③ a whole year after + S + V（在……整整一年後）
→ 適用：強調時間距離。
例：A whole year after the crisis began, the company finally recovered.
④ refuse to + V（拒絕做……）
→ 適用：描寫人物的決心。
例：The manager refused to give up despite limited resources.
⑤ With + N, S + V（在……情況下，……）
→ 適用：交代背景條件。
例：With little money, the team had to find a creative solution.
⑥ what was left（剩下的東西）
→ 適用：描述資源有限或災後情境。
例：They used what was left to build a simple shelter.
⑦ no one would have thought to + V（沒有人會想到要……）
→ 適用：強調創意或意外結果。
例：No one would have thought to turn the waste into a useful product.
⑧ at first..., but then...（一開始……，但後來……）
→ 適用：描述轉變。
例：At first the plan seemed risky, but then it proved successful.
⑨ shortened for + N（為了……而縮短）
→ 適用：說明命名或改寫原因。
例：The long title was shortened for the poster.
⑩ It wasn't born of A but of B（它不是源於 A，而是源於 B）
→ 適用：強調真正原因。
例：The movement wasn't born of anger but of hope.
⑪ to keep + O + V-ing（為了讓……持續……）
→ 適用：說明目的。
例：The volunteers worked hard to keep the shelter running.
⑫ Since then, S + has/have + p.p.（從那時起，……）
→ 適用：連接過去事件與現在結果。
例：Since then, the idea has spread across the world.
⑬ a range of + plural N（一系列……）
→ 適用：描述多樣性。
例：The program offers a range of courses for high school students.
⑭ as its name suggests（正如其名稱所暗示）
→ 適用：解釋名稱與特質的關聯。
例：As its name suggests, the app helps users focus.
